**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.9.6.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Panels are popovers now
- The menus are `UIPopoverPresentationController` popovers with a transparent `UIPopoverBackgroundView` (no arrow), `passthroughViews` for the toolbar, adaptive presentation disabled so they stay popovers on iPhone, and a `UIHostingController` sized to its SwiftUI content. No `GlassEffectContainer` or `glassEffectID` is needed. The circle-to-panel growth is iOS 26's popover presentation morphing out of the source view
- GlassPlayer's Tracks / Settings / Now Playing panels are now exactly that: UIKit popovers presented from the pill button that was tapped, with our glass card as content. Tapping outside dismisses; tapping another pill button switches. Works on iOS 17/18 too (system popover animation there)
- All the hand-rolled transition / scale / morph code is gone
