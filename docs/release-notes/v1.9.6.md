### Panels are popovers now — what Outplayer actually does
- From the Outplayer binary: its menus are `UIPopoverPresentationController` popovers (`VideoMenuPopoverViewController`, `OptionsPopoverViewController`) with a transparent `UIPopoverBackgroundView` (no arrow), `passthroughViews` for the toolbar, adaptive presentation disabled so they stay popovers on iPhone, and a `UIHostingController` sized to its SwiftUI content. There is no `GlassEffectContainer` or `glassEffectID` in it at all. The circle-to-panel growth is iOS 26's popover presentation morphing out of the source view
- GlassPlayer's Tracks / Settings / Now Playing panels are now exactly that: UIKit popovers presented from the pill button that was tapped, with our glass card as content. Tapping outside dismisses; tapping another pill button switches. Works on iOS 17/18 too (system popover animation there)
- All the hand-rolled transition / scale / morph code is gone
