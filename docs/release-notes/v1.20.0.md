**Install:** download `Folio-1.20.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

Nothing here has been tested on a phone yet. Each section says what the simulator showed and what to check. The full round report, with causes, evidence and device test steps, is in the Folio App project.

**Before testing:** turn on Settings → Help & Diagnostics → Diagnostics → **Trace library and images**, and **Trace Picture in Picture** for the PiP checks.

### Lock button grows into a pill
- The lock button at the top right is now a glass capsule that grows to the left into **Locked · Tap to unlock**, in the same style as the player's other pills. Tap the pill to unlock.
- It's one glass shape changing size. A second tap mid-way turns it back from where it is. With Reduce Motion it fades instead.
- The close and PiP buttons fade out where they are and nothing beside the lock moves. The bottom controls fade out while the player is locked.
- **Simulator:** the capsule grows from the round button over about 0.4 s, and its right edge stays put in portrait and landscape.
- **Changed from before:** unlocking used to be a separate pill at the bottom centre. It's now the pill at the top right.

### Real-Debrid has its own section on Home
- **Library** now shows only the files on this phone, and its count matches. The Real-Debrid tile and its collage are gone.
- **Below Library:** "Real-Debrid", the item count and size, and **See all** (the full library), then a row of your 20 newest releases, one per title, with posters and clean titles. A release opens the same page as in the Real-Debrid library.
- The section shows when your account's last check worked, stays (marked Offline) when Real-Debrid can't be reached, and goes away when the login is refused or the server is removed.
- Library's sort and layout menus apply to Library only.
- **Simulator:** Home's own share of launch wasn't slower (median 1235 ms before, 1059 ms after, 9 launches each).

### Folder tiles on Home
- **Each title once:** a folder's collage shows one picture per title in it. Two episodes of a show used to take two of its four pictures.
- **No more drawing past the tile:** after turning to landscape and back, a folder tile was drawn 11 pt wider than its column in every simulator run. Its collage is now a fixed 2 × 2 grid sized from its space, and the tile measures 111 pt in its 111 pt column every time.
- **Please check:** whether this is what made "Mixed" run over Classroom in your screenshot. The simulator never showed the full overlap.

### Picture in Picture: an Outplayer-style renderer to try
- Outplayer draws every frame straight into the layer that Picture in Picture shows, so starting and stopping PiP switches nothing. Folio's Metal renderer switches mpv's video output at each start and stop, and your 30 Oct log shows the picture stalling 177–236 ms and the sound stopping about 0.3 s at each switch.
- **New:** Settings → Player → Renderer → **OpenGL (being tested)** works the way Outplayer does. HDR and Dolby Vision show at normal brightness on it for now.
- **Metal stays the default.** The simulator can't run this path (no PiP, and its OpenGL texture cache refuses the frames), so it's untested anywhere. Please switch to OpenGL to test PiP.

### Faster stream starts with subtitle addons
- A stream waits at most 3 s for subtitle addons, then plays. Subtitles that arrive later are added to the playing file, so none are lost. Your log had two 20 s waits.

### Also
- **Title pages:** episode rows give the title the whole row: tag and size on the first line, the title below, then the synopsis in Details.
- **IMDb:** a page already downloading is shared rather than downloaded twice.
- **Text decoding:** text in metadata is decoded exactly once.
- **Library index:** the saved Real-Debrid index is read from disk once per launch.
- **Watch history:** Settings → Help & Diagnostics → Diagnostics → **Check watch history linking** counts how many of your history entries could be linked across Real-Debrid, your library and addons. It reads only and changes nothing. It's the first step of the shared history; nothing is migrated in this version.
- **Diagnostics:** new signposts for title pages and image decoding, and a launch line giving Home's own share of launch time.
