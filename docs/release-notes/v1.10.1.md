**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.10.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Fixed: panel stuck on screen and the player freezing
- Cause: each tap started opening or closing a panel immediately. Tapping again while a panel was still animating (another button, or the hidden button of the open panel) started a second transition on top of the first. UIKit then left a panel orphaned on screen with its transition never finishing, and it ignores touches while a transition runs, so the whole player froze
- Reproduced in CI by replaying the exact taps and timing from your log: on 1.10.0 the app froze and crashed inside UIKit's presentation code
- Now taps only record which panel you want, and the player gets there one step at a time: close what's open, let it finish, then open the new one. Taps during an animation are never lost; it goes straight to the last one. A short pause between steps fixed the last hang the replay found: a close started the instant an open finished never completed. A watchdog recovers if UIKit still doesn't report back
- With the fix, the same replay ends on the panel you tapped last, and the player stays responsive

### Delay before a panel shows
- The delay came from the same overlap: a new panel requested while the previous one was still closing waited behind it, or failed and appeared late. Now it opens as soon as the close finishes; switching panels fades the old one out in 0.12 s and the new one morphs out of the button you tapped
