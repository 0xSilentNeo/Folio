**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.11.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

Every fix here was measured before and after in the CI simulator, not just tried.

### Audio out of sync after hold-for-2× and PiP
- **Measured:** a new lab plays a clip with 5.1 audio in the real engine, holds 2×, releases, then switches renderer the way PiP does. With the audio output the app used (AVFoundation), holding 2× froze the picture while sound played, and on release the sound was 2.4 s ahead. The PiP switch froze the picture for about 3.5 s and left the sound 3.1 s off, taking about 10 s to settle
- **Fix:** the AudioUnit output is used first. The same lab with it: holding 2× ran within 0.17 s and release within 0.003 s; the PiP switches were within 0.15 s and settled in about 1 s
- The log now has a line after every speed or PiP change with the worst A/V offset and how long the picture stood still, so it can be checked on your phone

### Panels open without the lag
- **Measured:** building the Settings panel took 140–630 ms on the main thread before the animation could start, and every opening had 4–11 late frames because the content was laid out again on every frame of the morph
- **Fix:** each panel is built once, ahead of time while the player is idle, and reused. Its content keeps its final size during the morph instead of being laid out every frame
- **After:** tap to animation in 1–12 ms; Settings opens with 0 late frames (Tracks and Now Playing 1–6). Closing was already smooth
- The log now has a line for every panel open and close (frames, worst frame, late frames, time from tap)

### Native back on title pages
- The title page uses the system navigation bar (transparent): the system Back button and the real back swipe that follows your finger. The custom swipe from 1.11.0 is gone
- **Checked:** the test swipes from the left edge; the log shows iOS's own back gesture starting and finishing, and Back works before and after playing
