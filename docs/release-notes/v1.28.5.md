**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.28.5.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.28.4. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Kids profile passcode: like the Lock Screen
- **Face ID first, keys hidden.** While Face ID looks, the screen shows only its symbol, with Enter Passcode and Cancel at the bottom.
- **Then the passcode.** If Face ID doesn't recognise you, iOS's own prompt offers one more try; if that fails too, or you choose Cancel or Enter Passcode, the keys come in as they do on the Lock Screen. Face ID stays at the bottom left to try again.
- **Bigger keys.** They're now the Lock Screen's size: a fifth of the screen's width (88 pt on a Pro Max, 75 pt on a 375 pt phone), with its spacing. The circles grow with them.

### Notifications
- **Only the watching ones are reset when you open Folio.** Until now, opening Folio took back every pending notification and planned them again when you left, so one due while you were in Folio never came. Now only the ones that depend on what you do in Folio are taken back: Continue watching, Your library, Picks for you, and Just for fun's habit ones (opened without watching, a streak, the unfinished pile). Trending, the weekly briefing and new titles, and Just for fun's weather, holiday and Friday film night ones stay planned. If their moment comes while you're in Folio, they show over it.
- **New episodes were never reset.** They're the tracker's own notifications; since 1.28.2 they're planned again only when something changed.
- **Allow Notifications switch** at the top of Settings → Notifications. When it's off, none of Folio's notifications come, new episodes and finished downloads included. Each group keeps its own setting for when you turn it back on.
- **Pages show changes at once.** A city picked under Just for fun, a sound picked under Sounds, the Downloads switch changed from the downloads sheet, New episodes changed from Tracked Shows, and "Fewer Like This" from a notification now all show on the page underneath as soon as you're back. The cause was that these pages read the setting straight from storage when they were drawn, and nothing told them it had changed, so they kept the old value until they were opened again.

### Search
- **Clear Recent Searches** is now a row of its own at the end of Discover's recent searches. Before, it was only a small "Clear" in the list's header.

### Posters and widgets
- **Cowboy Bebop:** two series share the name and the folder has no year, so the poster stayed a placeholder. IMDb's popularity now breaks the tie: the 1998 series is far more popular than the 2021 one. Folders that were left unsure are tried again.
- **Real-Debrid's widget** now updates in iOS's background refresh. Before, it only updated when Folio read the library (at most every 10 minutes, and only while open), so Ramayana stayed on it after you removed it.

### Player
- **The play/pause morph is back,** a little quicker than SF Symbols' own pace.
- **Hot phone:** Anime4K uses its lighter stages from "fair" and pauses at "serious", with a note each way. In your report, it was taking 14–18 ms of every frame on top of software decoding, which is why frames dropped.
- **Software decoding:** a video the hardware decoder can't take now shows a note once ("Decoded in software"). Cowboy Bebop's Blu-ray is interlaced 1080i H.264, which iOS's decoder refuses, so the processor decodes it. That's expected for that file, not a fault.

### Control Centre
- The log now records whether iOS ever asks Folio to draw the Resume control, which shows whether the blank button is iOS not running Folio's control on this install.

### Tested
- Unit tests pass, including new ones for the poster tie-break, which notifications stay, the master switch, the settings pages and the key size. UI tests for the kids passcode, notifications and search pass in the simulator. Face ID itself can't be tried there, so please check the passcode on your phone.
