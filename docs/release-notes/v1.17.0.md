**Install:** download `Folio-1.17.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Watched
- **An eye marks what you've watched:** at the end of an episode row, and on a title's tile on Home when every episode in it is watched.
- **Mark single episodes watched or unwatched:** swipe an episode, long-press it, or use ••• → Mark this episode as watched. ••• also has Mark all as watched / unwatched. Unwatched clears the episode's progress.

### Swipe actions
- **Home:** swipe a title to the left for Delete; in List and Details view, swipe to the right for Watched / Unwatched. In Thumbnails view, a swipe to the left lays Delete and Watched over the tile.
- **Title pages:** swipe an episode or file to the left for Delete and Watched (over the tile in Thumbnails view). A long swipe in List or Details runs Delete.
- Delete asks first, from a swipe or a long-press, with the size. The question points at the title or episode it's about. Deleting a show's last files also removes its empty folder.

### Addon series
- **Next episode ready:** near the end of a streamed episode, the next one is found, its link and subtitles are fetched, and mpv starts buffering it, so Play on the Up Next card starts it right away. Setting: Playback → Get the next episode ready (on). Your debrid service adds the next episode early, even if you don't watch it; the setting says so.
- **Season check** (same setting): a few seconds after an episode from a season starts, the app checks which other episodes the same addon has in the same release (same binge group, or same name and resolution). In the playlist, episodes without that release say "Other release only · pick a stream", and those that aren't found at all say "Not available from this addon". When you play one with another release only, a stream list opens so you can pick.
- **Download instead of watch:** on an addon's stream list, swipe a stream to the left or long-press it → Download. The file goes into your library through the Downloads queue. Only direct links can be downloaded.

### Settings
- **One "mpv" page** for the renderer, picture, HDR, hardware decoding, cache, scripts, shaders, config files and logs. "Advanced" is now Help & Diagnostics.
- **Explanations under the settings** in General, Playback, Audio & Subtitles, Skip and mpv, e.g. what hardware decoding Copy-back does and what it costs.
- **Presets:** Settings → Presets saves all settings, WebDAV servers and mpv files to one file, and loads them back. Passwords, keys and addons (addon links usually contain your debrid key) are only included when you turn that on. Settings that an older or newer Folio version had but this one doesn't use are skipped, not an error.
- **Title pages: Full or Files only** (Settings → Library). Files only shows just the title and its files.

### Player
- **The next menu opens right away** after you close one or switch. iOS keeps a closing menu "presented" for about a second after it has visibly gone, and nothing else can open from the player meanwhile. The next menu now opens from a second, see-through layer. 1.16.1 had this turned off because iOS refused it: the menu was anchored to a button in the other layer. Anchored to the button's position instead, it starts opening 10 ms after the tap in the simulator, instead of about 1 s later. It shows up in the same place, but doesn't grow out of its button the way the first menu does.
- **Picture in Picture keeps the buffer**, going in and coming back (Metal renderer). Only mpv's video output is swapped now. Until 1.16.1 the video track was also switched off and on, and that makes mpv throw away what it had buffered and download it again.
