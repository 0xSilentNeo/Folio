### The delay before a title opens (mine, since 1.15–1.17)
- **You were right: it wasn't the animation.** Your phone's own log times every title open. The time from your tap until MobLand's page was built:
  - 1.14: 6 ms
  - 1.16.1: 96 ms
  - 1.17.0–1.17.4: ~220–320 ms
  - On 1.17.3 and 1.17.4, MobLand only started to move 470–665 ms after your tap; Dragon Ball (one episode) after ~90 ms.
- **The cause, from a CPU profile:** building the page looked through your whole watch history thousands of times.
  - Since 1.15.0 the page highlights the episode you watched last, and that was worked out again every time anything asked for it.
  - One line asked once for every file in your library.
  - 1.17.0 added more questions: the watched eye on every row and Home's rows.
  - Each look went through the whole history, twice for a file with no history entry.
- **Fixed:**
  - The history is looked up through an index.
  - The highlighted episode and the episode list are worked out once per change.
  - Home doesn't redraw itself every time a page opens or closes.
  - Home's library list is remembered until your files change.
- **Measured in the simulator** with a library and history like yours (MobLand's 10 episodes, Classroom's 12, Dragon Ball, ~50 history entries). From tap to MobLand's page built:
  - 1.17.4: 588 and 632 ms (median, two runs)
  - 1.17.5: ~170 ms
  - The simulator here is about twice as slow as your phone: it took 588 ms where your phone took ~290.
- Not tested on a phone yet. Your next log has a new line, "page opening: its move started", so it will show the time from tap to movement directly.

### Swiping back on a page that's still opening
- **Fixed: the page rebuilt itself at the moment you let go.** 1.17.3 added that, on top of what 1.17.2 already rebuilt at the start of every back. It happened exactly when iOS has to start sending the page back.
  - Your 28 Oct log: right after those let-gos, the log's 100 ms timer ran 170–610 ms late. That's where your recording's page hovers, vanishes and comes back.
  - Simulator profile: each let-go cost 380 ms of rebuilding on 1.17.4, and none now.
  - Touches on a leaving page are still blocked, the same way as before (UIKit's own switch on the page).

### Wrong title on a page (my 1.17.4 regression)
- **Fixed: Classroom opened with MobLand's title.** If you tapped a title while the last page was still going back, 1.17.4 swapped the new title into the old page, and SwiftUI reused that page.
- **Now the title you tap waits until the old page is gone,** a fraction of a second, then opens as a page of its own.
- Simulator, switching titles fast:
  - The tapped title opened every time, 16 of 16, with its own page and title.
  - The app's list of open pages never kept a stale page.

### Up Next
- **Fixed: after Up Next moved on, the time bar showed 0:00 left with the knob at the end,** and that episode got no Skip Intro/Ending. It happened when the next episode was exactly as long as the last one: mpv only reports a length when it changes, and the app had reset it to 0.
- **Reproduced and checked in the simulator** with two files of the same length:
  - Without the fix: the knob at the end, 0:00 (mpv said 0:40).
  - With the fix: 0:40 and a moving knob.
