**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.13.5.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Chips no longer cover the genres
When the chips under the title wrapped onto a second row (e.g. MKV on Classroom of the Elite), that row was drawn over the genres line. The chip row now reserves its full height, so a second row pushes the genres down. Applies to the library title page and the Discover page.

### Smaller download again
The IPA is compressed again (about 20 MB instead of 54 MB). The app's size on your phone is the same either way. This build also tests whether kSign installs a compressed IPA: if it fails to extract, use 1.13.4.
