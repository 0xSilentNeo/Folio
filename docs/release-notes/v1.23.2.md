### Autoplay at the end of an episode
- **What your report shows:** every Real-Debrid episode of The Gentlemen opened with "continue: 1 item(s) in queue". Each episode is a torrent of its own, so each sits in its own folder, and the queue was the files of that folder: one. At its end there was nothing to go on to, so the player closed. Local episodes went on as before (Classroom of the Elite E12 → E13 in the same report).
- **Now:** when the playing episode is the last in its queue, Folio looks for the show's later episodes in the server's saved library, in any folder, and adds them: the same release group first, then the same resolution. Up Next, getting the next episode ready, and autoplay then work as in a season pack.
- **Also fixed:** a next episode got ready in advance had its download address written into the episode itself, so history and Continue Watching kept an address that expires. Your report shows one played from Continue Watching on 5 Nov at 18:05, as a queue of one. Folio keeps those addresses apart now, and an entry saved that way finds its WebDAV address again by file name.
- **Tested in the simulator** with a local WebDAV server holding three episodes in three folders: "next episodes: 2 from 2 folder(s) … added", the Up Next card at the Ending chapter, E1 → E2 → E3 by itself, each opening in about 200 ms.

### Real-Debrid opening and seeking
- **Measured:** from this network, Real-Debrid's WebDAV host answers 0.74 s after a new connection, and Folio's own log shows its redirect to the download server takes 1.9–2.3 s each time ("link answered HTTP 206 in 2.2 s (redirected to syd3-4.download.real-debrid.com)").
- **Why it added up to 9–10 s:** mpv goes back to the address it was given whenever it reconnects, and is redirected again each time. Your library's files store tags at the end of the file, which mpv reads while opening ("Seeking to … to read header element 0x1254c367" in your mpv log). A resume reads the index at the end and then jumps, and every seek past what's buffered reconnects.
- **Now:** Folio follows the redirect once and gives mpv the download address. If that address stops working, Try again goes back to the WebDAV one. Continue Watching opens the player at once and lists the folder alongside, instead of first (that took 1.2 s).
- **Measured in the simulator** against a lab server that redirects with a 0.75 s delay: a resumed file went from 3.7 s to its first picture to 1.5 s; a fresh open stayed at 1.4 s.
- **Needs your phone:** how long Real-Debrid files take now. Each network file now writes an "open timeline" line to the log: every connection, seek and step, with times.

### Buffering and seeking in downloaded files
- **What your mpv log shows:** seeks in your local files took 0.14–0.43 s. The spinner showed after 350 ms, so the slower ones flashed it although nothing was loading (the 0.43 s one was waiting for the sound to line up).
- **Now:** for downloaded files the spinner waits a second. Slow seeks and any real wait for data are logged with the cache's state, so the next report shows where a pause comes from.

### Skip intro
- **Up Next named after the part:** its second button said "Watch Credits" over anything. It now says Watch Ending, Watch Preview and so on, from the file's chapter. In your report, the card on Classroom of the Elite came up at the chapter named "Ending".
- **No card over a scene after the credits:** an epilogue or post-credits scene (more than 30 s after the credits that isn't a preview) plays, and the next episode starts when the file ends. A preview after the credits keeps the card; a scene between the credits and a preview moves it to the preview.
- **Intros you watch:** watch an intro through to its end in two episodes of a show without skipping it, and that pill stops showing for that show. A toast says so, and Settings → Skip Intro & Credits → Intros you watch lists the shows and brings it back ("Show again"). The player's menu has "Show Skip Intro again" for the show playing. Tested in the simulator: E1 and E2 watched through, and E3 opened with "intro pills hidden: intro, opening".
- **Live-action shows treated as anime:** every episode file carries an anime hint, so a live-action show got anime lookups and Opening/Ending names (MobLand in your log). Only an anime database's id, IMDb's Animation genre or a title IMDb doesn't know count now.
- **An episode Up Next moved on from** counts as played through. It stayed "in progress" when its credits and preview were more than 8% of it.
- **Chapters added for skip markers** carry the pill's name, so an intro that sits at the end isn't labelled "Opening".
