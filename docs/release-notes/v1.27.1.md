**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.27.1.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.27.0. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### HDR
- **Dolby Vision films no longer turn green in Balanced and Enhanced** (OpenGL renderer). Those modes copy each frame out of the hardware decoder for all 10 bits, and the copies carried the film's Dolby Vision metadata, which the OpenGL renderer can't apply: it read the picture as Dolby Vision's own colours (Dune, a profile 7 remux, 9 Nov recording). While copying, that metadata is now left out and the film shows as its HDR10 base layer, as Efficient already showed it. The Metal renderer applies Dolby Vision and keeps it.
- **4K HDR that copying can't keep up with goes back to the hardware's frames.** Dune at 60 Mb/s dropped 85 frames in 20 s at 40–52% CPU and over 1 GB of memory. Folio now counts dropped frames while copying; more than one a second for ten seconds and it switches to the hardware's own frames (8-bit steps, HDR brightness kept) for the rest of the file, and says so.
- **Discover's grid loads without waiting on tips.** It waited on a tip's "Discover opened" event, which never finishes when tips aren't set up (the UI tests); it now counts in the background.

### Discover
- **Cinemeta or TMDB for the whole grid.** Addons has a new section, Discover's Titles: install Cinemeta in one tap, or add your TMDB key (moved here from Settings → Discover), and Discover's Trending, Top Rated and New come from them, page after page, past IMDb's 250. Drag to choose which goes first; the next is used when it doesn't answer, and IMDb when neither is set up. An "In Use" mark shows which one Discover uses.
- **No debrid libraries on Discover.** A debrid addon's library catalog (Torrentio's "RealDebrid") isn't a row any more, nor in On Discover; Cinemeta's catalogs aren't rows either, since it can be the grid.
- **Addons are checked for updates each launch**: a changed manifest (version, catalogs) is picked up, your switches and order kept.
- The Addons sheet slides up, as the Watchlist's does.

### Share sheet
- **Folio is in the share sheet's app row, with its icon.** Play in Folio was an action whose icon was blank; it's now Folio's share extension: a link from Safari or Messages plays, and a video from Files or Photos is moved into Folio's Media folder and played.

### Player
- **Pinching out of Fill no longer pulls the picture away from the top and bottom of the screen** (OpenGL renderer, 9 Nov recording). The pinch shrank the Fill layer, cropped to its view; it now shrinks Fit scaled up to look like Fill.
- Tune UI is gone from Advanced.

### Everywhere
- **Zoom between tabs is on for everyone**; its switch is gone.
- **Notifications → Sounds shows a sound as soon as you've picked it.** It kept the names it opened with.
- Home's "+" no longer has Add WebDAV Server (Network has every server).

### Tested
- Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.5): Discover's grid from Cinemeta, three pages (48, 47 and 50 titles); a shared video moved into Media and opened; the share extension registered with iOS. Unit tests: 508 pass. UI tests: Notifications, Kids passcode, Now Playing and Search tab pass.
- Not checked on a phone: the Dolby Vision and dropped-frame fixes (the simulator can't play that file), the pinch, and the share sheet itself.
