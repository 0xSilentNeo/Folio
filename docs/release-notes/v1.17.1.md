**Install:** download `Folio-1.17.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Crash going to the Home Screen
- **Fixed: the app was killed when you went to the Home Screen while a video was playing** (your 27 Oct 17:38 report; also 24 Oct). Here's what happened:
  - Going home starts PiP, and Folio switches mpv's video output, which ends mpv's Metal video thread.
  - When a thread ends, iOS applies the layer changes it made, on that same thread, including any layout UIKit had waiting. The snapshot iOS takes as you leave leaves exactly such a layout waiting.
  - UIKit's layout engine refuses to run off the main thread, so the app was killed. Your crash stack shows every step of this.

  The 1.12 fix took the video view out of the window first. That didn't prevent it, and removing the view itself left a layout waiting.
- **What changed:**
  - Right before anything that ends that thread (PiP starting, video going off in the background, closing the player), Folio now does the waiting layout on the main thread first. Closing the player ends the thread right away too, instead of during mpv's shutdown afterwards.
  - As a safety net, UIKit layout requested from any other thread is moved to the main thread.
- **Tested:** in the simulator, a thread that changes a view's layer and then ends crashes the app the same way: same signal, and with symbols, the same path (thread exit → Core Animation commit → window layout → layout engine exception). With the safety net, the app logs it, does the layout on the main thread and keeps running. I couldn't make mpv's own thread trigger it in the simulator, even with a layout forced to wait as PiP starts, so the first part is untested there. If it ever catches one on your phone, the log will say "layout of … asked for on a background thread".

### Player menus
- **Every menu morphs out of its button, and the next one opens straight away.** This is Outplayer's own sequence, read from its app binary (the menu presenter at 0x1000c3308): whatever menu is up, even one still closing, is dismissed at once, and the new one is presented in the same call. Folio used to leave a closing menu to finish, and iOS opens nothing new from the player until it has. That meant waiting about 1 s (1.16), or, in 1.17.0, a second layer whose menus animated differently. That layer is gone. In the simulator, a menu asked for 0.1–0.4 s into another's close starts its morph 10 ms later. When you switch while a menu is fully open, iOS holds the new one about 0.2 s, same as in Outplayer.
- **A tap on a menu button just as another menu finishes closing now opens it.** While a menu closes, iOS hasn't given the buttons back yet, so a layer over the player takes those taps. That layer went away the moment the close ended, even with your finger still down, and the tap was lost. The simulator's touch log caught it: finger down 21 ms before the close ended, up 44 ms after, and nothing opened. The layer now stays until the finger lifts. In a test where each press started during a close and ended after it: 5 of 6 opened before, 6 of 6 now.
- **Tapping to the right of a menu closes it at once.** Those taps landed on the video (the screen's right-hand margin) instead of the layer that closes menus, and the video waits about 0.35 s to rule out a double tap. Measured in the simulator: 350–410 ms before, 0 ms now.

### Picture in Picture
- **No more replaying the last second** going into PiP and coming back. After swapping its video output, mpv jumps back to the last frame it *showed* (its playback time follows the picture). When the picture had stalled, that was behind the sound. Folio now continues from where the sound is and logs both positions.

### Coming back to a paused video
- **No more sound over a still picture for 5–6 s** when you press Play after the app was away while paused. iOS ended the hardware decoder in the meantime. Your 27 Oct mpv log shows the invalid-session error, the missing reference frames, and the fallback to copy-back until the next keyframe. On return, mpv now builds a new decoder and redraws the current frame from the buffer; nothing is downloaded again.

### Title pages
- **The page's ‹ and ••• can't stay over Home any more, and don't disappear from the page.** They were the system navigation bar's buttons. Home hides that bar, so every zoom into or out of a title page also showed or hid it, and iOS gets that wrong when the zoom follows your finger:
  - On Apple's developer forums (thread 774524), an Apple engineer confirmed it's a bug: after a swipe back, the page's toolbar can stay on the screen below. That's your 26 Oct recording.
  - A react-native-screens report (#5054, iOS 27) traces the other case in UIKit: when a swipe back is let go, the bar stays at zero opacity. Reproduced on 1.17.0 in the simulator: after 6 of 6 let-go swipes, ‹ and ••• were gone and the log showed the bar at alpha 0.

  Title pages now hide the bar just like Home and draw ‹ and ••• themselves, in the same places (same size and glass). They're part of the page, so they come and go with it. Tested in the simulator: they stay after 6 let-go swipes; after 8 quick swipes back, some before the page had finished opening, Home showed neither and opened the next title each time; swiping back still works; and both buttons respond across their whole circle.
- **Settings and folder pages keep the system bar.** When a swipe back is let go there, the bar is now made visible again. On 1.17.0 it stayed invisible: in the test, all 4 let-go swipes on Settings hit this, and each was fixed.
- **Taps while a page is still moving in or out are ignored.** 0.4–1.2 s after a page opened, the 27 Oct log has taps meant for Home playing an episode row. Page buttons now ignore taps while the page is moving, and the log records each move and every ignored tap.

### Addon seasons
- **Picking another release works.** The stream picker now opens inside the player. 1.17.0 tried to open it as a sheet while the Now Playing menu was open, which iOS doesn't allow, so it never appeared.
- **The episode you're watching stays the current one until the next has loaded**, whether you tap it in the playlist, press Next, or Up Next moves on. 1.17.0 switched first and stopped what was playing. With the picker up, its progress was then saved under the wrong episode, and the end of the file skipped further. Now nothing moves on by itself while the picker is open, and a lookup that finishes after you've chosen something else is ignored. Tapping the row of the episode that's playing does nothing (it used to reload it).
- **Up Next no longer skips episodes.** When its countdown ended while the next episode was still being looked up, it fired again and again: six times in your log, from E1 to E6.
- **The next episode gets ready 2 minutes before the Up Next card**, not only near the end of the file. With 4 minutes of credits, the countdown used to finish before anything was ready.

### Swipe actions
- **Watched and Download buttons are readable** (a dark tint instead of the cream accent under a white icon).
- **Watched and Delete work on swiped episode rows** (reproduced on 1.17.0 in the simulator: the tap only closed the row; passes now). The invisible "tap to close" layer stayed where the row had been, on top of the revealed buttons. Per Apple's `offset` docs, modifiers applied after an offset stay at the view's original place.
