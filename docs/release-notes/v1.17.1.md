**Install:** download `Folio-1.17.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player menus
- **Every menu morphs out of its button, and the next one opens straight away.** This is Outplayer's own sequence, read from its app binary (the menu presenter at 0x1000c3308): whatever menu is up, even one still closing, is dismissed at once, and the new one is presented in the same call. Folio used to leave a closing menu to finish, and iOS opens nothing new from the player until it has. That meant waiting about 1 s (1.16), or, in 1.17.0, a second layer whose menus animated differently. That layer is gone. In the simulator, a menu asked for 0.1–0.4 s into another's close starts its morph 10 ms later. When you switch while a menu is fully open, iOS holds the new one about 0.2 s, same as in Outplayer.
- **Tapping to the right of a menu closes it at once.** Those taps landed on the video (the screen's right-hand margin) instead of the layer that closes menus, and the video waits about 0.35 s to rule out a double tap. Measured in the simulator: 350–410 ms before, 0 ms now.

### Picture in Picture
- **No more replaying the last second** going into PiP and coming back. After swapping its video output, mpv jumps back to the last frame it *showed* (its playback time follows the picture). When the picture had stalled, that was behind the sound. Folio now continues from where the sound is and logs both positions.

### Coming back to a paused video
- **No more sound over a still picture for 5–6 s** when you press Play after the app was away while paused. iOS ended the hardware decoder in the meantime. Your 27 Oct mpv log shows the invalid-session error, the missing reference frames, and the fallback to copy-back until the next keyframe. On return, mpv now builds a new decoder and redraws the current frame from the buffer; nothing is downloaded again.

### Title pages
- **Taps while a page is still zooming in or out are ignored.** Your 27 Oct log has episode rows playing 0.4–1.2 s after their page appeared, from taps meant for Home. In the simulator, a page takes touches for its whole zoom-in (about 0.9 s). The log says when a tap was ignored.

### Addon seasons
- **Picking another release works.** The stream picker now opens inside the player. 1.17.0 tried to open it as a sheet while the Now Playing menu was open, which iOS doesn't allow, so it never appeared.
- **The episode you're watching keeps playing** while you pick from the playlist. 1.17.0 stopped it as soon as you tapped the other episode. Tapping the row of the episode that's playing does nothing now (it used to reload it).
- **Up Next no longer skips episodes.** When its countdown ended while the next episode was still being looked up, it fired again and again: six times in your log, from E1 to E6.
- **The next episode gets ready 2 minutes before the Up Next card**, not only near the end of the file. With 4 minutes of credits, the countdown used to finish before anything was ready.

### Swipe actions
- **Watched and Download buttons are readable** (a dark tint instead of the cream accent under a white icon).
- **Watched and Delete work on swiped episode rows.** The invisible "tap to close" layer stayed where the row had been, on top of the revealed buttons. Per Apple's `offset` docs, modifiers applied after an offset stay at the view's original place.
