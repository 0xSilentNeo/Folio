**Install:** download `Folio-1.20.2.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Read this first: 1.20.2 installs as a new app
- Folio has a new app ID, `dev.silentneo.folio`, without the old name in it. iOS treats it as a different app, so 1.20.2 installs **next to** the Folio you have, not over it, and starts empty.
- **Before installing**, in the Folio you have now:
  1. Settings → Presets: turn on **Include passwords and keys**, then **Save settings to a file…** and keep the file in Files.
  2. Files → On My iPhone → Folio → **Media**: copy any files you want to keep somewhere else in Files.
- **In 1.20.2:** Settings → Presets → **Load settings from a file…**, then copy your media files into Files → On My iPhone → Folio → Media.
- What carries over that way: every setting, your WebDAV servers and their passwords, your TMDB key, your addons and your mpv files (mpv.conf, input.conf, scripts, shaders). **Watch history and Continue Watching don't**: they start fresh.
- Then delete the old Folio. Until you do, both apps answer `folio://` links, and iOS picks one.

### Fixes
- **Next episode at the wrong time.** Next, a stream pick and auto-next switched to the new episode before the last one's place was saved, so the last episode's time and length went to the new one's history, and it opened there. History now always goes to the file that's actually open.
- **"q" on a keyboard.** mpv's own "q" (quit) key, `quit` in the console or from a script left the player on screen with nothing behind it, and could crash on the OpenGL renderer or during PiP. The player now closes as with its close button. Tested in the simulator: "q" closes the player, the app carries on, and the next video plays.
- **Short clips counted as watched.** Anything with less than 90 s left counted as finished, so a clip under 90 s was "watched" before it began. That rule now applies only to videos of 10 minutes or more.
- **Every next episode was downloaded twice.** "Get the next episode ready" asks the link for its first byte so Real-Debrid prepares the file. But Real-Debrid sends the whole file anyway, and the app kept all of it in memory: your 1 Nov log has "HTTP 206, 885 MB in 17216 ms", then 891, 977 and 926 MB, once per episode. That likely explains the memory warnings and the app closing while the next episode loaded (27 Oct). Now only the answer's headers are read and the rest is cancelled.
- **`folio://` links.** They were ignored; only links with the old name worked. Now `folio://play?url=…` and `folio://addon?url=…` open Folio, and the old scheme is gone.
  - An addon link now **asks before adding** ("Add this addon?" with its address). Any web page could add one silently before. A failure now says so.
  - A play link only plays network streams (http, https, rtsp, rtmp, smb, sftp, ftp), and links with any other action are ignored.
- **Keys in diagnostics reports.** Addon links that carry a debrid key in plain text (`realdebrid=…`, `alldebrid=…`, `torbox=…` and others), API-key headers and other long key-like values are now hidden. The report's settings file is filtered too, and settings named like secrets (your TMDB key) are left out. Settings → Diagnostics → Last log now shows and shares a filtered copy of mpv's log.
- **Crash records.** After a crash with a reason (an exception), the record kept only the backtrace, with the reason overwritten (your 1 Nov report: "signal 05" and nothing else). The reason now comes first and the backtrace after it.

### The old name, and other apps' names
- The old name is gone from the app, its code, its settings text, mpv's default files, the project, CI and the docs. Other apps' names are gone from the code comments and release notes.
- Past releases on GitHub have new titles, notes and file names to match. Commit messages from before stay as they were.
