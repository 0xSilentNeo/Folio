**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.27.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.26.3. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Addons, a source of their own
- **A puzzle-piece button at the top of Discover opens Addons**, the sheet growing out of it (it was an item in a "…" menu). Streaming Services is a button beside it.
- **Each addon has a switch.** Off, it keeps its link but isn't asked for streams, subtitles or catalogs.
- **Each addon says how it last answered**: "Streams in 420 ms, 2 minutes ago", or "failed after 20 s" in orange.
- **The list's order is the streams list's order**, and the page now says so: tap Edit and drag.
- **Installed addons' catalogs are rows on Discover**, above the grid, in the addons' order; each one can be hidden under On Discover. Not shown in the Kids Profile.
- Removing an addon asks first. An addon's empty rating no longer shows a lone star on its posters.

### First run
- **A welcome sheet on a new install**, with three cards: Add Your Videos (opens Files), Connect a Server (asks which kind) and Add Addons (opens the Addons sheet on Discover). Below them, the player's gestures.
- **Tips where they apply**, at most one an hour: Addons on Discover's button after a second visit with none installed; servers at the top of an empty Network page; the gestures in the first plays, gone once you use one.

### Play in Folio
- **Share a link and choose Play in Folio** (Safari, Messages, anything that shares a web link). Folio opens and plays it. If iOS doesn't let it open Folio, the link waits and Folio offers it the next time it's opened (within 10 minutes).
- **Play URL in Folio** in Shortcuts: for the Action Button, a Home Screen shortcut, or a shortcut shown in the share sheet. Siri: "Play a link in Folio".

### Downloads
- **The Dynamic Island comes back.** When iOS declined Folio's background progress once (the 9 Nov report), Folio never asked again until it was relaunched; it now asks again when you come back to it, and a minute after a refusal.
- **Higher priority in the background**: the transfers and the writing run as work you're waiting for (they ran as utility work, which iOS slows the most).
- **The widget shows a speed only while it's current** (90 seconds). With Folio in the background iOS lets it rewrite the widget every few minutes, and an old speed read as the speed now (20.4 MB/s while the island said 60).
- The report now has a line a minute in the background (speed, files, whether the phone is locked) and the HTTP version each server answers with.
- **The downloads pill no longer covers Discover's filter bar.** It rises above it while Discover is on screen.

### Player
- **A single file plays alone.** A film with nothing else in its folder no longer queued the library behind it, and ends without an Up Next card.
- **Media Info says where the sound goes and why it has fewer channels**: "Stereo · iPhone speaker" and "5.1 mixed down to stereo: the iPhone speaker takes 2 channels." An Atmos track notes that its height objects aren't decoded.
- **Connecting HDMI or an AirPlay receiver mid-film reopens the audio output** for the new route's channels (it stayed as it started).
- **The chapter name shows over the knob while you drag**, above the controls' glass (it was cut off by it).
- **No buffering bar for files on the phone.**
- **Tighter Settings panel**: "Skip" with a menu (5–60 s) instead of "Skip inter-val" on three lines; delays and speed on one line ("0.0 s"); Advanced has mpv commands and UI tuning out of the way.
- **VoiceOver**: controls stay up while it runs, every button says what it does, a two-finger double tap plays and pauses, toasts are read out. Reduce Motion: fades instead of slides and zooms, and no pulsing.

### Everywhere
- **Settings' explanations open without jumping**: the two-line text is swapped for the whole text, which iOS animates as a row, so the groups below slide instead of jumping.
- **Discover's filter bar fits small phones** (iPhone 13 mini: icons for Sort and Genre when the words don't fit) and every chip is at least 44 pt tall.
- **Movies and Series are the same control everywhere**, in the same order: Discover, the streaming services' pages and the Watchlist.
- **Empty and error pages are iOS's own**, with Try Again where it helps (IMDb, servers, folders).
- Text grows with Dynamic Type where it was fixed (Discover's and title pages' headings, the Kids pages, the Play button). Continue Watching cards are buttons for VoiceOver; headings are headings; ratings read as "Rated 8.2".
- **Deleting a server asks first**, saying what goes with it.
- About no longer has a Source Code link.

### Tested
- Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 13 mini, iOS 26.5): the welcome sheet and its Add Addons card, the Addons sheet (install, switch, timing, On Discover), addon catalogs on Discover, a shared link opening the player, a link left over offered on the next open, Discover's header and filter bar on the 13 mini.
- Unit tests: 501 pass (new: audio route notes, addon switch and catalogs, shared links, first run). UI tests: Notifications, Kids passcode and Now Playing pass. The project as CI generates it (xcodegen) builds Play in Folio and the widget for the phone.
- Not checked on a phone: the share sheet's Play in Folio itself (the simulator's Safari wouldn't open its share sheet for the tools), background download speeds, and audio routes (HDMI, AirPlay, AirPods).
