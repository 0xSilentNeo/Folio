**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.30.2.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.30.1. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

A new search page, four fixes to 1.30.1's own changes, and the rest of the code review's findings.

### Search, redone
- **One kind of row for everything.** A picture, the title, one line under it, and a small badge saying where you have it: Library, your server's name, an addon's. No badge means it's on IMDb only. Before, the library, servers and IMDb each had their own rows and sections.
- **A show on a server is one row**, not a row per file ("MobLand · 10 files"). It opens the folder; a single file plays. "Open folder" is in the row's menu and swipe.
- **The best match of what's yours comes first, as a card with Play or Resume.**
- **All · Yours · Movies · Shows** under the field replace the section headers and the Files/Posters switch. "All" shows the first few of each with "Show all".
- **Addons' answers join the same list** (no more carousel per catalog), without repeating a title IMDb already gave.
- **One quiet status line** instead of the spinner rows and "Keep typing".
- **Before you type:** your recent searches as chips (hold one to delete it) and a Continue watching row. The magnifier and the explanation are gone.

### Fixes to 1.30.1
- **Plays waiting for a sync service are no longer dropped while you're offline.** 1.30.1's change to keep them through outages dropped them after 60 tries without a connection.
- **Next no longer plays against your pause** after a stream picker that opened at an episode's end was cancelled.
- **A title page keeps its details between two episodes** instead of going blank until the lookup answered.
- **The volume fallback works from the keyboard again.**

### Player
- Events from mpv are ignored once the player is closing; a late one could start the next episode or skip an intro in a player on its way out.
- The mpv log is read from its end when a stream opens (it was read whole, every time, and grows all session).
- **Enter, < and > go through Folio's queue** (next and previous). Sent on to mpv they changed file inside it, and history was saved under the wrong episode. Your own input.conf binding for those three keys alone no longer applies; with a modifier it does.
- "Try again" after a stream stopped early goes on from where it stopped, also with Resume off.
- The audio session of a player that closed can no longer be let go after the next one has started.
- A track list read for the last file can't land in the next one's; show memory is written only when it changed; iOS's media services restarting sets the audio session up again.
- VoiceOver's escape closes an open panel or card before the player; chapters at the same time both show; the time bar keeps its seek track at the largest text sizes; coming back from a sheet doesn't turn the player to landscape again.
- Metal: a frame the engine asked about and didn't draw no longer holds up leaving the app; the texture cache is emptied when the frame size changes.

### Security and privacy
- **Opening a .lua, .glsl or .hook file asks before installing it.** A failed copy no longer deletes the one already there.
- **A WebDAV login is only sent to the server it's for.** Links the server gives to another host, or to plain http, are rebuilt on your server's address.
- Paused downloads' resume files (they hold the login) are kept out of backups.
- A server's address is looked up for an icon at DuckDuckGo and Google only for the built-in presets.
- The share inbox only takes files from its own folder.

### Downloads
- Low Power holding a download gives it time to write what arrived before iOS's task ends.
- A finished file can't be saved twice; a full disk is said so at once instead of after the retries; speeds keep updating while you scroll; "Use mobile data" applies when switched.
- Saves are written in order; the background session is made once; a restarted connection that had already finished no longer leaves a file at 100% "running".

### Library, servers, Discover
- **Every SMB, Plex and Jellyfin folder no longer gets a sort of its own just by being opened.**
- **A quick look into a watched episode keeps it watched**; past the first minute it's being watched again and resumes as before.
- Opening a file in a big WebDAV folder reads the Keychain once, not once per file.
- Posters are decoded at the size they're shown; server pictures try again after a failed fetch.
- Continue Watching's queue uses clean titles and the right subtitles; a failed lookup can't save the wrong kind of match.
- The Streaming Services region is saved on Done, checked, and Cancel leaves it alone.
- TMDB failures are told apart from empty lists; a year-only episode date isn't 1 January; an anime is matched to the IMDb title of the right year; a cancelled page load can't answer the next one.

### Tracking, alerts, sync, settings
- A tracked show's "has it ended" check runs once a week, and not in the background.
- A play is not sent twice to a service within 12 hours; a token is refreshed once, not by two requests at once; disconnecting during a refresh stays disconnected.
- Alerts are recorded as each is scheduled; a custom sound with more than two channels is converted instead of ending the app.
- Presets leave out the test switches and include appearance, sounds and the notification groups.
- No empty Experimental section before iOS 26; a new option file needs a name; the kids lockout doesn't end when the clock is moved forward.

### Widgets
- Fewer timeline entries and only the pictures a widget's size draws, and the blurred backdrop is made once. This is aimed at the widget crash in the reports (its cause isn't proven).
- The inline Lock Screen widget's messages have their background set.

### Removed
- A few unused functions and settings keys.

### Known and left
- The player's main file is not split up, lab code and the lab clip still ship in the app (the CI labs run on the release build and need them), and the two engine ideas (reusing the frame's texture, 10-bit HDR frames) are not done: each needs a phone to check.
- Logins inside a pasted link's address, and a stream's own headers, are still saved with history.
- The widget crash needs a 1.28.11 symbol file, or a report from this build, to be sure.

### Tested
- Unit tests pass (607, 24 of them new: the search's grouping, caps and chips, WebDAV link rebuilding, the watched mark, the sent-plays record, presets, the kids lockout and more).
- Simulator UI tests pass for search, launch and settings search; the new search was looked at there in light and dark.
- **Not checked on a phone:** nothing in this build has run on one. The simulator here can't start the Metal renderer, so every player and Metal change is unrun, as are downloads, the widgets, sync, the WebDAV and Keychain changes, the sound import, and search against a real server library (the simulator's had one show).
