**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.9.9.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Panels grow out of their buttons — measured, not assumed
- 1.9.8's device trace showed the popover still presented with no transition even from a glass button. So the panels no longer use a UIKit presentation at all: each is our own Liquid Glass view (`UIVisualEffectView` + `UIGlassEffect`) in the player window, animated with plain UIView springs — the one kind of animation the device traces show running
- Open: the glass starts as the tapped button's circle and springs to the panel size (with a small overshoot), the content fades and scales in inside it. Close: the content fades, the glass shrinks back into the button. Switch: shrink into one button, grow out of the other. Tap outside closes; the pill stays tappable
- Lab trace of the new path: `46×46 → 144×107 → 265×182 → 326×220 → 344×232 → 340×229`, 22 distinct states over ~0.5 s on the glass view. The same trace is written on the device
