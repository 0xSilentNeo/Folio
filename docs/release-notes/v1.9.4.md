### Panel animation — the Liquid Glass morph
- Frame-by-frame, the panel should not be scaled in: its **glass shape** starts as a circle at the button and morphs into the rounded rectangle while the content fades in behind. That is the iOS 26 Liquid Glass morph (`glassEffectID` inside a `GlassEffectContainer`)
- The controls and the panels now live in one glass container. Each panel button carries its own glass circle (the three circles are unioned into the pill), and the panel's glass shares that button's id — so opening morphs the button's circle into the panel, closing morphs it back, switching panels morphs from one button to the other. Below iOS 26 the panel scales out of the pill as in 1.3
- **The doubled UI in your recording was a real bug: a second copy of the panel view had survived in the code since 1.9.0, so two panels were drawn at once.** Removed
- Spring response/damping from Tune UI still apply; start scale is gone (the morph defines the shape)
