**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.12.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Discover title page, redesigned
- Backdrop with the poster beside the title; the title deciphers in, with the show's own romaji title under it for anime
- Synopsis with **+ more**; one **Find streams** button under it — the 3D tilt button from the library page. On a series it reads *Play Season 1* or *Continue Season 1 · from episode 4*; every episode row still has its own streams
- **Cast** with headshots: actor and role, from the IMDb title page; tapping one opens their IMDb page. Settings → General → *Cast on title pages*: above the episodes or at the bottom (default)
- **Wikipedia** and **IMDb** buttons. The Wikipedia article is found by title and year through Wikipedia's own search
- The library title page gets the same cast strip and links (titles already looked up fill their cast in on the next visit)

### 3D play button
- Small tilt only (±6°, half that from the phone's motion); a light sweep crosses it every 4 s with a glow that breathes. Nothing animates in between, and the sweep stops while a video plays over the page

### Panels
- Switching panels by tapping another pill spot cross-fades in place: the pill no longer flashes between them
- A tap on a panel's own spot that lands right after it closed is never dropped (lab: 20/20 at 0.5 s, 10/10 rapid)
- The full panel test suite (open/switch/close/outside tap/segmented control/long press/rapid/replay/lock/title-page Back) passes in CI

### Heat and battery
- New `[energy]` line in the log every 30 s while the app is in front, and whenever the phone's thermal state changes: this app's CPU use split by thread (`main`, `mpv/vo`, `mpv/vd`, `webkit`, `audio`…), thermal state, and while playing the renderer, picture mode and decoder. This is what the next round of cuts will be based on; please send a log after a long play session
- The IMDb warm-up at launch only runs if Discover was used in the last week and the phone is not in Low Power Mode
- Discover grid tiles no longer carry a blurred drop shadow (each was re-rendered on every scroll frame); the play button's glow is a gradient, not a blur filter
- The hang watchdog wakes half as often and sleeps in the background; the button's motion sensing runs at 20 Hz and only while the page is on screen

### Also
- PiP: closing it with ✕ while the app is already back in front no longer leaves a black picture with sound running
- Library tiles use the poster (Settings → Library → *Tile artwork* to go back to video frames); saved frames live in Application Support so they survive a Caches purge
- English episode titles on IMDb lookups (the web-view path lacked an Accept-Language header)
- A swipe that starts at the very bottom or top edge in landscape never seeks
- IMDb: timings in the log for every page (plain HTTP or web view), stale-first lists, smaller grid posters, title page details and seasons fetched in parallel
