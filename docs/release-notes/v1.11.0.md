**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.11.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Panels: a tap where the pill was just closes the panel
- While a panel is open the pill is hidden (its glass became the panel), and its buttons no longer react. Any tap outside the panel, including where the pill was, closes it; then tap the button you want
- Checked in CI: with Tracks open, a tap where Settings was closes Tracks and opens nothing; same for Now Playing; a tap on its own spot closes it too

### Title page: Back and the swipe back
- **Back button:** the button was the interactive glass circle with no tap area of its own. The glass took the touch, so the button only reacted sometimes. The whole circle is now the button. The same fix applies to every glass button in the app
- **Swipe back:** never worked on title pages. iOS turns off its back swipe when a page hides the navigation bar, and these pages draw their own Back button. Title pages now have their own swipe: from the left edge to the right
- **Navigation:** title pages are opened through the app's own navigation list, so going back no longer depends on the Home list underneath staying the same
- **Orientation:** after you close the player with the phone held upright, the library returns to portrait (it could stay in landscape, with taps landing in the wrong place)
- Checked in CI: open a title, Back; open it again, play an episode, close the player, Back; then the edge swipe. On 1.10.1 neither Back nor the swipe worked in this test; now all three do

### Audio & Subtitles
- The screen is split into **Audio** and **Subtitles** sections
- **Preferred audio language** and **Preferred subtitle language** open a list: add languages, drag to set the order, swipe to remove. The first language a file has is used. (The audio default was, and still is, Japanese first, then English)
- "Show subtitles" (on/off) is now clearly a subtitles setting

### Discover: Play Season
- On a series page: **Play Season N**, or **Continue Season N · E4** if you've already watched some episodes
- Pick a stream for the first episode as usual. Each later episode's stream is found just before it plays, from the same addon, preferring the same release (Stremio's "binge group"), then the same resolution. Debrid links don't expire while waiting, and nothing is asked up front for the whole season
- If no matching stream is found for an episode, the player says so and you can pick one from the title page

### Startup animation
- The app icon's glass tile springs in, the pink play button pops onto it, then it zooms past you into Home. It runs while Home loads underneath and never holds launch back; with Reduce Motion it is a short fade

### Launch speed
- The library is now listed off the main thread (it used to run before Home could draw), and file names and file details are read once per scan instead of on every redraw
- The log now records how long launch took ("first screen … ms after process start"), and whether iOS had prepared the app in advance

### Also
- More space between Continue Watching cards
- The title page's tilting Play button stops reading the motion sensor while a video plays or the app is in the background (it was redrawing 30 times a second under the player)
