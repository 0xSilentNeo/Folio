### WebDAV: sorting, and the grey menu items
- **The grey items were really disabled.** "Ascending", "Clean titles" and "Only media files" couldn't be tapped: the UI test read each one as disabled.
  - The cause: the app's own switch style was applied to the toggles inside the menu. With the system style they work.
- **Sorting:**
  - The sort button's menu has only **Recently Added, Name, Size**, with a check on the current one.
  - The arrow next to it turns the order over (it flips round as it changes).
  - Picking a sort starts it the natural way round: newest first, largest first, or A to Z.
  - **Clean titles, Only media files and Download all** are in the new **More** (•••) menu.
- **How each sort works:**
  - **Name:** sorts by the name you see, Finder-style: "Episode 2" before "Episode 10".
  - **Recently Added:** uses the date the server says the item was created. When it doesn't send one, it uses the last-changed date, which a debrid service doesn't change after adding. Items with no date go last.
  - **Size:** files by size. Folders have no size, so they stay by name.
  - **Folders** are first in every sort.
- **Your choice is remembered** for each folder. The last one you picked is the default for folders you haven't sorted.
- **Sorting doesn't reload anything from the server,** and the order is worked out once per change, not on every redraw.
- **Simulator, against a test server:** every button enabled; Name, Recently Added and Size in both directions in the expected order; Only media files works; the choice survives a relaunch.
- **Please check:** open a big Real-Debrid folder, sort by Recently Added, turn it over, and switch to Size. The app log now has one line per server showing which dates and sizes it sends (`[webdav] properties from …`).

### Addons timing out
- **Not fixed yet: it needs measuring first.** The addon request settings haven't changed since at least 1.14.1 (20 s timeout). What is new since 1.17.0 is extra traffic to the same addon (below), so that's still a suspect.
- **What I could check:**
  - Your two addon servers answer in under half a second from outside (manifests only; your addon addresses carry your keys).
  - Since 1.17.0 the app also asks your addon for each later episode (season check) and warms up the next episode's link.
- **Every addon request is now timed** in the log: how long it waited to go out, DNS, connection, TLS, and how long the server took to answer. Failures and requests over 5 s are always logged.
- **Please:**

  2. Open a few episodes' streams until one times out, then send the report.
  3. For comparison, turn off **Get the next episode ready** for a while and see whether timeouts stop.

### Picture in Picture
- **From your recording, frame by frame:**
  - **Going in:** the PiP window opened on a shot from a few seconds earlier, then jumped to the current one.
  - **Coming back:** a frame from when PiP started flashed for about 0.07 s.
  - **Both ways:** about 0.25 s of complete silence.
- **Fixed (untested on device):**
  - PiP gets the current frame as you leave the app, and when you tap the PiP button.
  - Coming back, the PiP picture stays in front until the player has shown a new frame after the switch.
- **Not fixed: the silence.** Each switch (GPU in the app, CPU in PiP, because iOS allows no GPU work in the background) makes mpv rebuild its video and seek, and that seek empties the sound buffer. Removing it means changing mpv itself, which I'll propose separately.
- **Your log also shows the app came back in landscape,** as 1.18.0 intended. The turn in the recording is iOS animating the PiP window into the landscape player.

### Title page
- **The romaji under the title is back.**
  - The saved copy of IMDb's data left the original title out. The first lookup had it, and every later lookup read the saved copy without it.
  - Titles are fetched once more so saved ones get it back.
  - For anime: the romaji, or nothing. Never the episode name in its place.
  - Other shows still show the episode name.
- **The title wraps** to up to three lines instead of being cut, and follows your text size. At the largest sizes it moves under the poster and gets up to five lines.
- I couldn't reproduce the one-line cut in the simulator, even with your three-line episode name. Please check Classroom of the Elite's page.

### Skip Opening came late
- **Your log:** on four episodes in a row, the skip times arrived exactly 15.5 s after the file opened. The first service (IntroDB) ran into its 15 s timeout before the second (TheIntroDB) was asked.
- **Fixed:** both are asked at the same time. IntroDB still wins if it answers within 2 s of the other.
- **Please check:** the Skip Opening button should be there from the start of the opening.

### Next episode ready before Up Next
- **Your log:** Play Next on the card still loaded for 3.3 and 5.8 s.
  - mpv opens the next file only once the current one has been read to its end, and it reads ahead only as far as the buffer allows.
- **Now:**
  - Getting the next episode ready starts 4 minutes before the card (was 2).
  - The buffer limit is raised to fit the rest of the file, so mpv can open the next one before the card. It's capped by the memory iOS gives the app, and skipped after a memory warning.
- **Please check:** with an addon or WebDAV series, wait for the card, then tap Play Next. It should start at once. The log line `up next card at …` says whether it was ready.
