**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.22.4.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.22.3 and has everything in 1.22.2 and 1.22.3 too. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Search beside the tab bar
- **A search button of its own beside the tab bar,** the way Apple's apps have it: on iOS 26 it opens into the search field at the bottom, with the keyboard up. It's there on every page, Home included.
- **One field for everything,** in this order:
  - your library's titles ("In your library"; in the kids profile only the titles it allows);
  - the files on your WebDAV and Real-Debrid servers ("On your servers", from each server's index, which "Search all files" used; not in the kids profile);
  - titles on IMDb and your addons, with recent searches, as Discover's search had.
- **Discover's magnifier is gone,** since the tab bar has the search now.
- Plex and Jellyfin aren't in it yet: they need testing against a real server.
- iOS 17 has a plain Search tab instead.

### Zoom between tabs (an experiment)
- Settings → General → **Zoom between tabs**, off by default. The page you switch to grows into place as it fades in, and only fades with Reduce Motion. The tab bar itself stays put.

### Please check

- A search across your Real-Debrid server, and opening a result.
- The zoom, if you turn it on.
