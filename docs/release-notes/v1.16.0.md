**Install:** download `Folio-1.16.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player menus (Native menu morph)
- **The system morph stays:** the menus grow out of their button with Apple's Liquid Glass animation.
- **iOS 27: nothing waits for the animation.** Tap another menu button while a menu is open and that menu opens straight away. Tap outside, or the same button, and the menu closes at once, even while it is still opening. Tapping a button while a menu is still opening switches to that menu. These are the rules Outplayer uses on iOS 27.
- The menu buttons are built like Outplayer's (one glass button each; 38 pt on iOS 27).
- On iOS 26 the menus behave as in 1.15.1: a tap made during the animation is acted on when it ends.

### Under the hood
- Built with Xcode 27 and the iOS 27 SDK.
