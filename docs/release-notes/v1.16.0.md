**Install:** download `Folio-1.16.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player menus (Native menu morph)
- **iOS 27: switch menus with one tap.** With a menu open, tap another menu button and that menu opens straight away. The open one is gone at once, with no close animation to wait for. Tapping a button while a menu is still opening also ends on the new menu.
- Menus keep Apple's Liquid Glass morph out of their button.
- The menu buttons are one glass button each, 38 pt on iOS 27. The toolbar is wider on iOS 27, which otherwise tucks Settings and Now Playing into a "⋯" menu.
- On iOS 26 the menus behave as in 1.15.1.
- Known limit, from iOS itself: a tap made while a menu is still *closing* is held until the animation nearly ends, and sometimes dropped (seen on the iOS 26 and 27 simulators).

### Under the hood
- Built with Xcode 27 and the iOS 27 SDK.
