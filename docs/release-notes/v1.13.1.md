**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.13.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Panels: the button morph is back
- 1.13.0 replaced the button → panel morph with a fade ("Quick"). Your recording showed it also drew **two panels** when re-opening one (my shrink-out and a second grow-in on screen at once). Quick is gone; the system morph is the only animation again
- Kept from 1.13.0: one tap anywhere outside a panel closes it (including where the pill is), and the fix for the black full-screen panel
- **A tap during the close:** Apple's docs for bar-item popovers say iOS *replaces the button until the panel is dismissed* — so a tap on the pill while it was still closing hit the animating glass (your "bobble") and was lost. The player now keeps catching taps while a panel closes, and a tap on a button's spot opens that panel as soon as the close ends. **Not verifiable in CI** (the test runner can't tap fast enough to land inside the close) — please try it
- Checked in CI with the morph: one tap outside closes 9/9; tap outside while a panel opens 10/10; a button tap after a close opens 20/20; rapid open/close 10/10; the freeze-log replay; title-page Back

### Buttons that needed several taps
- On iOS 26 an icon-only toolbar button reacts only to taps on the **glyph**, though a full circle is drawn around it (Apple Developer Forums thread 815492). The ⋯ on title pages is a thin row of dots — hence hit-or-miss. Its whole circle is tappable now. **Checked:** five off-centre taps on ⋯ open the menu 5/5
- Same fix for the WebDAV sort and search icons and the Settings "+"; the Discover filter chips (only their text was tappable); "+ more"; the small menu chevrons in the player panels

### Title pages
- Chips no longer run into the description: the title was placed with `offset`, which (per SwiftUI's docs) doesn't change layout, over a fixed 44 pt gap. The header now takes its real height
- File size / file name row under Play removed
- Play button: tilt 7°, the bright highlight and the coloured glow removed (the light sweep is much fainter)
