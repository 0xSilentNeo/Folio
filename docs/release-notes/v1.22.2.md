**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.22.2.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.22.1 (same app ID, so your library, history and settings stay). It's a pre-release: it becomes a release only once you've checked it on your phone. Please try the list under "Please check" and send a report (Settings → Help & Diagnostics, with the PiP trace switched on).

### Fixed, and checked in the simulator
- **The kids passcode is chosen once and kept** until you change it. Turning the profile on again asks for nothing; turning it off asks for the same passcode. Change Passcode is there when the profile is off too. The keypad has glass keys like the Lock Screen's, and nothing under the circles but what went wrong.
- **Player controls: one width scale, the same on both sides.** 80% is 78 pt from each edge, 90% 39 pt, 100% 16 pt, 60% 156 pt, in the top row and the bottom row alike. In 1.22.1 only 80% was 78 pt, and the others kept the old scale.
- **The menu buttons no longer cover the time bar.** With the controls wider than 80% their capsule reached into the screen's side safe area, and iOS moved it left over the end of the time bar. Folio now also places the capsule by where iOS actually draws it.
- **Quick skips:** a tap while a skip is still landing joins the next seek, and the sound stays off until the picture is there, so it no longer cuts in and out.
- **Sharper Picture in Picture with OpenGL:** frames at the video's own size (at most 1080p). 1.22.1 shrank them to a quarter, 480×270 for a 1080p episode.
- **Track only on shows still running.** IMDb's end year decides: Game of Thrones (2011–2019) has none, Classroom of the Elite (2017–) has it. A show you already track keeps the button, so you can stop.
- **Episodes not out yet don't open the streams page,** where nothing could be found.
- **Dynamic Island:** no play symbol next to a pause symbol. Without the episode's picture there's a film icon, and a paused video says "Paused".

### Looked into, from the 4 Nov report
- **For you in the kids profile** was filtered as set: IMDb rates Dragon Ball Z, Black Clover and Dragon Ball Super TV-PG, and Dragon Ball Super: Broly PG, all within "TV-PG and PG-13". It hid the ones rated TV-14 (Dragon Ball Daima, One Piece, Dragon Ball). Lower the limits in Settings → Kids Profile for a stricter row.
- **Widgets:** the log showed no App Group on every launch, 1.22.1 included. With a certificate of its own the signing tool gives Folio its profile's groups, so Folio now tries those too, and the log says which one it uses or what the profile lists.
- **Control Center showing "Not Playing":** the cause isn't found yet. When a video starts, the log now records the audio session and the Now Playing commands.
- **The stutter after Notification Centre (Metal):** a full pull sends Folio to the background, and Metal then rebuilds its video output on return. That code is the same as in 1.21.0. With the PiP trace on, the log now records the picture and the main thread for 4 s after each trip away, and after a Control Center pull.
