**Install:** download `Folio-1.20.3.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.20.2 (same app ID, so your library, history and settings stay).

The IPA now carries a widget, which is an app extension. With a free Apple ID that uses one more App ID. If your sideloading tool asks whether to keep app extensions, keep them to get the widget.

New: SMB, Jellyfin and Plex, settings remembered per show, night mode and dialogue boost, subtitle styles and search, a Continue Watching widget with Siri, and "For you" in Discover. Also more than 40 fixes from the 2 Nov code review. Nothing here has been tested on a phone yet.

### New
- **SMB shares, Jellyfin and Plex.** In the Network tab, under your WebDAV servers: "Add SMB, Jellyfin or Plex".
  - **SMB:** a host (nas.local or an address), an optional share, and a user name and password ("DOMAIN\user" works; leave both empty for guest access). Without a share, Folio lists the host's shares. Videos play straight from the share, with the subtitle files next to them.
  - **Jellyfin:** the server's address, your user name and password. Folio signs in once and keeps only the server's token; your password isn't stored.
  - **Plex:** the server's address and your Plex token (the form says where to find it). Signing in with a plex.tv account isn't supported yet.
  - Continue Watching plays on with the rest of the folder or season. Passwords and tokens stay in the iOS Keychain: they're never written to history, and the log hides them.
- **Remember per show** (Settings → Playback, on by default). The audio and subtitle tracks, subtitle and audio delay, Fill and Anime4K you pick in the player are used again for the next episode of that show, or the next time you watch that film.
  - Tracks are found by language and name, so it works when episodes number their tracks differently.
  - A subtitle delay is also kept per file.
  - "Remembered shows" in Settings lists them. The player's Settings menu has "Forget this show's settings".
- **Night mode and dialogue boost.** In the player's Settings menu under Audio; Settings → Audio & Subtitles sets what every video starts with.
  - Night mode lowers the bass and LFE, where most of a film's loudness is. Dolby Digital (Plus) tracks also use their own range compression, from the next file or audio track.
  - Dialogue boost raises the centre channel, where the dialogue is, and lowers the rest on 5.1 and 7.1; on stereo it raises the voice frequencies.
  - Your mpv.conf audio filters stay as they are.
- **Subtitle styles and search.**
  - Styles: Default, Large, Yellow and Boxed, in Settings → Audio & Subtitles and in the player's Settings menu. Plain-text subtitles (SRT, WebVTT) take the style; styled ASS subtitles keep their own look.
  - "Search subtitles…" in the player's Tracks menu asks your subtitle addons for the title playing. A library or WebDAV file is looked up by its name first.
- **A Continue Watching widget** for the Home Screen (small and medium) and the Lock Screen. Tap a show to resume it. If your sideloading tool doesn't register Folio's App Group, the widget says to open Folio instead.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** "Continue watching in Folio", and "Play the next episode of *show* in Folio" for any show in Continue Watching or your library.
- **"For you" in Discover:** titles like the ones you've watched, from IMDb's "More like this", plus TMDB's recommendations when you've added a TMDB key. Titles you've already watched are left out. It shows once you've watched something with a known title.

### Things that could lose your data
- **Test connection no longer changes a server's saved password.** It wrote the typed password to the Keychain before testing. A wrong password, Test, then Cancel left the server with the wrong one: "Login refused" and failed downloads. Now Test uses the typed password only for that test, and Save saves it.
- **The mpv.conf, input.conf and script editor no longer wipes files it can't read.** A file that isn't UTF-8 text (an older Latin-1 `input.conf` or `.lua`) opened as empty and was saved empty when you left. Now it says "Can't edit this file here" and leaves the file alone. The editor also stopped rewriting files you only looked at.
- **Delete asks first in a folder's List, Details and Thumbnails views,** as it does on Home. Deleting from there now also removes the file's picture and tells Home.
- **Start over no longer erases the resume point first.** It used to delete the title's history before the player opened, so a file that failed to open lost its place and watched mark. Now the player starts at 0 and history updates as you watch.

### Things that showed or did the wrong thing
- **Offline no longer blanks posters and names for 3 days.** A failed IMDb search was remembered as "no such title" for 3 days. Now only real answers are remembered.
- **Two shows' "Season 1" folders no longer download into one.** A season, Extras or Specials folder now downloads into a folder named after its show ("Mob Land – Season 1"). Before, same-named episodes from two shows replaced each other.
- **A downloaded file you deleted no longer shows as downloaded,** and can be downloaded again.
- **Discover: tapping a season while a title page loads keeps that season.** The first season's episodes could arrive later and replace it, leaving the list empty and the button on "loading episodes…".
- **Names:** "Toy Story 4" and "Apollo 13 (1995)" are films, not episode 4 and 13; "Ocean's 11" has no season 11; "Blade Runner 2049 (2017)" is from 2017. Films like these now get the right poster, and skip times for films are asked for as films.
- **Skip markers from a file's own chapters win again.** The skip lookup often ran before the chapter list was read, so the online lookup was used even for files with Opening/Ending chapters. Now it waits for both the length and the chapters.
- **A swipe that iOS cancels no longer acts.** A long swipe on a download, cut short by a call or the app leaving, ran Cancel with no question. Now it springs back.
- **Loop mode stays on.** A saved Loop setting was applied only when changed, so a new video ignored it.

### More fixes from the review
**Library and Home**
- **Delete on a series page names the episode** ("Delete S1 · E5"), and offers "Delete All 12 Episodes". It used to delete the next-up episode, whatever it was, and close the page.
- **A series page's season chips follow the episode** after a season finale.
- **Deleting a folder from Home counts everything in it.** The question counted only the videos, but the subtitles, pictures and other files went too.
- **Taps during the launch animation no longer reach Home** underneath and open a title nobody saw.
- **Tiles with the same date or size keep their order.** They could swap places between refreshes.
- **Private server names (a NAS, *.lan, *.home.arpa, a tailnet) are no longer sent to icon services** for a logo, and a public server with no logo is asked once a week, not on every row.
- **A title without a picture keeps the same placeholder colour** from one launch to the next.
- **Continue Watching pictures can't be shared by two files** whose long paths end alike. Saved pictures are moved to the new names on first use.

**Discover, addons and skip times**
- **One odd entry from an addon no longer empties the whole list.** A catalog, an episode list, the streams or the subtitles used to vanish because of one item; a malformed search or genre option hid the addon's search and genres.
- **Installing an addon again with a new configuration replaces it** instead of adding a second copy.
- **Searching for a title with a "/" in it** (Fate/Zero, AC/DC) no longer fails with "Couldn't reach IMDb".
- **A title page's countries, plot and year come from that title,** not from a related title on the same IMDb page.
- **Leaving a title page while it loads no longer keeps the bare preview** (no cast, plot or runtime) for good.
- **Skip times:** a lookup that failed (offline, a server down) is tried again after 10 minutes instead of never in that session, and the large anime timestamps file isn't downloaded again for every episode after a failure. Later seasons match whole words only: "II" no longer matches "III".
- **English episode titles:** TVmaze being unreachable is no longer remembered as "show not found" for a day.
- **Wikipedia** opens the search page when no article matches the title, instead of keeping a wrong first result (an actor, another film) for good.
- **"&#0;" and similar codes in titles** no longer turn into invisible characters or "�".

**Player**
- **The track list can't miss a change:** a refresh asked for while another was running was dropped, so a newly added subtitle could be missing or the wrong track marked.

**Downloads and servers**
- **File names from a server are cleaned** ("../", "/" and ":" can't place a file outside Media), and a file already there is never replaced: the new one gets " 2".
- **A download that failed with bad resume data starts afresh** on Retry instead of failing for good. Retry waits are kept, and "wrong password" or "not found" answers aren't retried over and over.
- **Changing download settings takes effect at once**, not at the next battery change.
- **Removing a server cancels its unfinished downloads**, which went on retrying with no login.
- **A saved entry this version can't read no longer loses the whole download or server list.**
- **WebDAV folders remember a sort only when you pick one**, not for every folder you open.
- **Subtitles for "Ep 1" no longer include "Ep 10.srt".**
- **Loading a preset with a `null` in a setting no longer crashes the app.**
- **A shader or script installed from a link can't be saved outside its folder** through a name like "..%2F".
- **Passwords are changed in place in the Keychain.** They were deleted and added again, so a failed save lost the password, with nothing in the log.

**Logs and the report**
- **Clear logs clears everything** (the previous log and PiP pictures too) and logging carries on; before, the session had no app log until the next launch.
- **A full disk no longer crashes the app** on its next log line, and the app log is cut down during a session instead of growing until the next launch.
- **Filtering secrets out of log lines no longer runs on the main thread.**
- **An mpv log with a non-UTF-8 file name in it is no longer left out of the report.**
- **Posters:** an error page saved as a poster is no longer kept (and failing) for good; it's downloaded again. A reused row no longer shows the previous title's picture while the new one loads.
