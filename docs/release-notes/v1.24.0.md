**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.24.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.23.4. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Widgets, redesigned
As in the 6 Nov mockup. Each widget has one look that follows the Home Screen appearance you pick in iOS (long-press the Home Screen → Edit → Customize):
- **Light and Dark:** Folio's paper and ink. Automatic switches with the system.
- **Clear and Tinted:** iOS draws these itself. Per Apple's guide to accented rendering and Liquid Glass, it removes the widget's background and colours everything by transparency alone. So in those looks:
  - artwork turns grey and faint, and your tint colours it;
  - the dark shading under text goes, since iOS would turn it into a white haze;
  - text on a button is cut out of the button, so it stays readable.

**Continue Watching**, four widgets:
- **Continue Watching:** small (the still, minutes left, play), medium (the last one big, plus Up next), large (the last one, plus three more).
  - Widgets you already placed keep working; they're this one.
- **Progress:** small; a ring with the minutes left, and the episode's name.
- **Next Episode:** medium; Resume, plus a button straight to the next episode.
  - The next episode's number shows when Folio knows it without asking a server: library files, and files in a server's saved library (Real-Debrid included).
- **Continue Shelf:** large; six posters with their progress. With three or fewer, one row with each title's place under it.

**Airing**, three widgets:
- **Airing:**
  - small: a countdown that ticks on its own within a day, the day further out, then "Out now";
  - medium: This week, with today highlighted;
  - large: a week strip with a dot on airing days, then the episodes in order.
- **Airing Day:** small; the next episode's day on the show's poster. An episode with no known hour says the day, never a made-up time.
- **Out Now:** medium; the newest episode out this week.
  - **Play** shows when Folio found it in your library or on a server.
  - A tap looks the file up again, so the widget never holds a file's address.

**New widgets:**
- **Downloads** (small): how far, how fast, how long. Idle: what's downloaded this week.
- **Your Week** (small): time watched in the last seven days, with shows, films and episodes.
  - Counted on your phone from now on (a small watch log; nothing leaves the phone).
  - Until a week has passed, it says which day it started counting.
- **New on Your Server** (medium): the four newest releases in a server's saved library, with their quality. Real-Debrid first.
  - A tap opens the release's folder.
- **Pick for Tonight** (medium): a film in your library you haven't started.
  - **Play** plays it; **Shuffle** shows another (a new order each day).

**Lock Screen:**
- Continue Watching: rectangular, circular (minutes left) and above the clock.
- Airing: rectangular ("Tonight 9:00 PM", and what's next), circular (a countdown ring) and above the clock.

**Control (iOS 18):** **Resume**, for Control Center, the Lock Screen's buttons or the Action button. One tap opens Folio and plays what you watched last from its place. It's built as Apple's guide asks for a control that opens its app: an "open" action that's in both Folio and its widget.

**Empty and broken states:**
- Nothing to continue says so, with Browse.
- Nothing tracked says "Track a show".
- No access: "Open Folio once", or "Can't reach Folio" when the signing gives the widget no way in.

### Reminders (engagement notifications)
The lines you approved, sent from your phone. Settings → **Reminders** turns them off.
- **Rules:**
  - At most one a day.
  - None between 10 pm and 9 am.
  - Never two about the same show within three days.
  - Never while the kids profile is on.
- **Permission:** not asked at launch. If notifications aren't allowed yet, Reminders has the button.
- **When:** planned each time Folio leaves the screen, and removed when you're back in it.
- **The moments:**
  - paused mid-episode (two days on);
  - left on a cliffhanger (the next episode is in your library);
  - a week away;
  - the houseplants one (12 days);
  - after a late night (12:30 that day);
  - a binge (4 or more episodes in a day);
  - a show nearly done;
  - a film nearly done;
  - Friday.
- **New episode out** and **download finished** reuse the existing notifications, in the new wording, in the day. A new season's first episode gets the darker line.
- **Not guessed:** "episodes left" (cliffhanger, nearly done, Friday) is counted only for shows whose files are in your library. For Real-Debrid and Discover shows those three don't fire.

### Tested
- **Unit tests:** all 440 in the simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 26.5). The new ones cover:
  - the watch log and Your week;
  - the next episode's label;
  - the Airing snapshot's recent episodes;
  - finding an aired episode by its show's names;
  - the new links and files;
  - older snapshots still reading;
  - 22 for the reminders' timing, texts and rules.
- **Every widget in every look:** a lab switch (`-labWidgets`) draws all 18 widget layouts and states in Light, Dark, Clear and Tinted, plus the Lock Screen ones, from what the app wrote for them. Checked one by one. Fixed from them:
  - buttons that stretched in Clear and Tinted;
  - "Today" written twice on the countdown;
  - rows floating mid-card;
  - an empty half on the shelf.
- **Reminders in the simulator:** all 12 delivered with the exact texts.
- **UI tests:** the same release set as 1.23.4, on a clean install.
  - All pass except PanelUITests 11, 40, 49 and 50, which fail the same way on 1.23.2's code in this simulator.
  - The first run also failed EpisodeRows (large text) and PanelUITests 32, 36 and 44. That was lab data left in the simulator: a tracked show airing that day and extra films pushed Home's library off the screen. On a clean install all four pass.
- **CI:** the IPA built. CI's unit-test run had five download-test timeouts, the kind it also had on 1.23.4, in code this build doesn't touch; all 440 pass here.

### Needs your phone
- **The Home Screen itself:** this simulator's widget gallery opens blank, even for Apple's widgets, so no widget could be placed on its Home Screen.
  - The lab drew the widgets' own views.
  - Clear and Tinted there are stand-ins: iOS does that colouring itself.
  - Please add a few, and switch the Home Screen between Light, Dark, Clear and Tinted.
- **The Resume control:** the simulator registered it ("Resume", an app launcher), but it wasn't tapped from Control Center.
- **The reminders' timing:** they arrive on later days, so their first ones come after you've used this build for a while.
