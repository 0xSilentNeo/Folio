**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.22.6.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.22.5 and has everything since 1.22.2. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### The search button, on iOS 27
- **Search now sits on its own beside the tab bar on iOS 27,** as a round button that opens into the field where it is. Cancel takes you back to the page you were on.
- **Why it didn't before:** the build is made with the iOS 27 SDK. iOS 27 sets a search tab apart only when choosing it opens its search field, and Folio's didn't say so, so iOS 27 drew Search as a fourth tab inside the bar (your screenshots). On iOS 26 it was set apart either way, which is all the simulator here could show. The search itself was in 1.22.5 as described.
- Checked on an iOS 27 simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max) before and after: before, Home · Discover · Network · Search in one bar; after, the round button beside the bar, the bar folding into a Home button while you type, and your library's results.

### Tracking shows that have ended
- **Dragon Ball no longer gets Dragon Ball Super: Beerus's times.** AniList calls a new series a "sequel" too, and Folio followed sequels when a season had finished: Dragon Ball → Dragon Ball Z → Beerus, which is airing. Now a sequel is followed only while the series hasn't ended (IMDb gives Dragon Ball 1986–1989), and only to a TV or web series, not a film or special.
- **Bleach** (IMDb: 2006–2012) no longer follows into Thousand-Year Blood War.
- **No Track button on a show that has ended,** by IMDb's years or, when IMDb hasn't caught up, by TVmaze: IMDb still has Thousand-Year Blood War as "2022–", TVmaze has it ended on 26 Oct.
- **Shows you tracked that have ended stop being tracked** once their last episode's week as "New" is over. Dragon Ball and Bleach go on the next refresh, or when you open their page.

### Episode dates
- **The newest episode keeps its date.** The "New" badge replaced it (Tulsa King S4E3 showed none).
- **A release with no known hour shows its day only.** TVmaze has no time for most streaming releases and puts them at 12:00 UTC, which was the "Airs tomorrow 11:00 pm" you saw in Melbourne. It's "Airs tomorrow" now.
- **Dates of episodes out in the last month or still to come are checked against TVmaze,** which follows schedule changes. Where the two differ, TVmaze's date is shown and the log says so. On 5 Nov IMDb had Tulsa King S4E5 on 14 Nov, TVmaze on 13 Nov (it's weekly, on Fridays). Not for anime, whose seasons the two number differently.
- Before this there was no check: IMDb's dates were kept for a week as they were.

### Streaming services' logos
- **Netflix, Prime Video, HBO Max, Disney+, Apple TV+, Paramount+, Hulu, Peacock and Crunchyroll show their own logos** on Discover, from TMDB's image server, shaped like app icons. Until a logo loads, or offline, the letters show as before.
