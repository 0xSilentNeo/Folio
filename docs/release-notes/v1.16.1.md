**Install:** download `Folio-1.16.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player menus
- The progress bar is full length again in landscape. 1.16.0 gave the menu buttons 236 pt for a glass capsule 154 pt wide; measured on iOS 27, they need 186 pt, and the empty part now sits outside the layout.

### Coming back to the app
- **The buffer is kept.** Leaving the app while paused no longer switches the video off. Switching it off made the player throw away everything it had buffered and download it again when you came back. When audio carries on playing in the background, video is still switched off (iOS doesn't let a background app draw video).
- **No more garbled picture after coming back** in a different orientation. The picture was rebuilt at the old screen size while the video was paused, and stayed that way until you pressed play.
- **Back to the player from Picture in Picture:** when you open the app while PiP is up, the stop request is now repeated once the app is fully open (it was sometimes ignored), and every step goes in the log.

### Library
- **Continue Watching:** a picture wider than the card no longer pushes the title and Resume off the card. Addon titles show a wide picture (episode still or backdrop) instead of a cropped portrait poster, and the row starts at the card you just watched.
- **Title pages in landscape:** the backdrop takes at most 60% of the screen's height instead of 360 pt of a 440 pt-tall screen, and is fetched at the screen's real resolution. Before, an episode still 480 px wide was stretched across 2868 px. The tab bar no longer sits on top of the title.
- **A title no longer starts playing when you swipe back from its page.** The Play button counted a touch as a tap when the finger moved less than 12 pt — measured relative to the button. During a back swipe the page, and the button with it, moves along with your finger, so a swipe looked like a tap. Movement is now measured on the screen. Taps that coincide with a back swipe are also ignored, and the log names the control that started playback.

### Other
- Other apps' names removed from Settings, the code and the release notes.
