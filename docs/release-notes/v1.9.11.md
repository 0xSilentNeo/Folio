**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.9.11.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Panels morph out of their button — verified before release
- Root cause of the missing animation since 1.9.6: the panels were popovers with a custom transparent background. Reproduced in a CI simulator experiment: with a custom popover background iOS 26 skips the Liquid Glass morph and the panel just appears; with the system background it morphs out of its source
- Outplayer's binary shows the construction it uses on Liquid Glass: its menu button is a `UIBarButtonItem` in a `UIToolbar`, the menus are system popovers, and its Playlist/Chapters switcher is a system `UISegmentedControl` with a tint colour. GlassPlayer now does the same:
  - the Tracks · Settings · Now Playing pill is a toolbar (iOS draws it as one glass capsule), panels are system popovers from those bar items: the capsule lifts off the bar as a glass blob and grows into the panel with its content inside; closing shrinks it back
  - panels sit above the bar, so tapping another button switches panels directly
  - the hand-made overlay from 1.9.9–1.9.10 is removed (it caused the content spilling outside the glass)
- iOS 17/18: same panels from the UIKit pill (system popover animation)

### Now Playing
- The Playlist / Chapters / Markers switcher is the system segmented control: tap or drag and the thumb lifts into a glass lens, slides, and settles in pink — the tab-bar behaviour. Same control for Skip interval and Movies/Series in Discover
- Content fits the panel exactly; fixed height

### How this was checked
- An automated UI test (XCUITest) now runs in CI on the iOS 26 simulator with the screen recorded: real taps open Tracks, switch to Settings and Now Playing, drag and tap the segmented control, and close by tapping outside. It asserts each panel appears, the other buttons stay tappable, and the panel closes. The recording was inspected frame by frame for the morph and the lens
