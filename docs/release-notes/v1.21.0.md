**Install:** download `Folio-1.21.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.20.3 (same app ID, so your library, history and settings stay). Keep app extensions if your sideloading tool asks: the widgets are one.

New: **track the shows and anime you're following.** Folio tells you when the next episode airs, in your own time zone, with a notification, a Home row, a widget and Siri. Also: Siri closes when the video starts, Real-Debrid appears on Home without a restart, the mpv files explain themselves, and 14 more fixes from the review. Nothing here has been tested on a phone yet.

### Track airing shows
- **A Track button** on a series' page, in Discover and in your library. Tracked shows are listed in Settings → Tracking: airing today, this week, later, and ended or unknown. Each has its next episode, its local air time and a countdown, and a link to its TVmaze or MyAnimeList page. Swipe a show to stop tracking it.
- **Schedules:** series come from TVmaze and anime from AniList. Both give each episode's exact air time; IMDb and MyAnimeList don't. When an anime season ends, Folio follows it to the next one.
- **Notifications** when a new episode is out, or an hour before ("New episodes" in Settings, under Tracking). Folio asks for permission the first time you tap Track. Tapping a notification opens the show.
- **"Airing soon" on Home,** under Continue Watching: episodes out now or airing in the next 7 days. It doesn't show when nothing you track airs that week.
- **An "Airing" widget** in every size, Lock Screen included. It counts down on its own and turns to "Out now" at air time, without opening Folio.
- **Siri:** "When's the next episode of *show* in Folio?" answers out loud without opening the app.
- Schedules are checked when you open Folio (if older than 6 hours), when you pull to refresh, and in the background when iOS allows.

### Siri
- **Siri goes away when the video starts.** "Continue watching in Folio" and "Play the next episode of …" used to answer out loud, and Siri's orb and card stayed over the player. Over a full-screen video you often couldn't swipe it away without leaving the app. Now the player opening is the answer, and Siri only speaks when there's nothing to play.

### Real-Debrid on Home
- **Saving a server checks its library at once.** Home used to look again only when the address, user name or folder changed. After fixing a wrong password, the Real-Debrid section stayed away until you restarted the app.
- **While the library is read the first time, Home says so:** "Reading your Real-Debrid library… 40 of 213 folders".
- **A refused login says so,** with an Edit button, instead of the section quietly not appearing.
- **An empty library is one line** when you have a Real-Debrid account, so the Real-Debrid row sits near the top instead of below a page of instructions.
- **A show's season packs are one tile** in the Real-Debrid row ("Show S01" and "Show 2025 S02" showed as two).

### mpv.conf, input.conf, scripts and shaders explained
- The files Folio puts in the mpv folder now explain what you can do with them:
  - **mpv.conf:** languages, subtitle look and loading, audio, picture quality, smoother motion, streaming, screenshots and profiles. It also lists what Folio sets itself and which options to leave alone.
  - **input.conf:** mpv's own shortcuts, key names, and examples for seeking, sound, subtitles and the picture.
  - **Scripts and shaders READMEs:** what Lua scripts can and can't do on iOS (with two small scripts to start from), and where shaders run.
- Files you've never edited are updated to the new versions. Files you've changed are left exactly as they are.

### Fixes
**Player**
- **Brightness goes back** to what it was when you close the player. A swipe used to leave the whole app at the player's brightness.
- **Play after seeking back from the end continues from there,** instead of starting the video over.
- **A seek drag cut short** (a call, the app leaving, the lock) no longer freezes the time label or stops the controls from hiding.
- **Closing the player within a moment of opening it** no longer leaves the app showing nothing.
- **Retry only shows when it can work.** With mpv failed to start (an invalid option in mpv.conf) it did nothing; that mpv is now freed too, instead of running until the app quit.
- **A subtitle file that can't be opened says so**, instead of being added as nothing.
- **Screenshot says "failed" when it failed,** and saving it no longer freezes the player for a moment.
- **A shader with ":" in its file name** no longer stops every shader from loading.

**Library**
- **Deleting a file or folder also removes its Continue Watching card and progress,** and the saved frames of everything in a folder. The card used to stay and fail to play. Progress is matched by the file's place, never its name, so a same-named file elsewhere keeps its own.

**Discover**
- **"New" ignores a genre picked under another sort.** "New" has no genre menu, so the list was filtered out of sight with no way to see why.
- **A missing IMDb page or an IMDb server error no longer goes through the slow hidden browser** (up to 25 s each, holding up other pages). Only IMDb's bot check does. Offline, nothing is tried.
