**Install:** download `Folio-1.20.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

Nothing here has been tested on a phone yet. Each section says what the simulator showed and what to check. Picture in Picture and a video playing on in the background can't run in the simulator.

**Before testing:** turn on Settings → Help & Diagnostics → Diagnostics → **Trace Picture in Picture**, and **Trace library and images** for the library.

### Notification Centre: the picture comes back as it slides away
- **Your 1 Nov log, Notification Centre at 20:12:**
  - iOS said the app was coming back at 20:12:10.97, and the app became active 0.85 s later.
  - The player waited for "active" to bring its video output back. The seek that follows showed the spinner, and the picture moved again a second after Notification Centre started to go.
- **Now** the video output comes back as soon as the app starts coming back, while Notification Centre is still sliding up.
- If the video comes back on the software decoder (iOS still holding the hardware one), it's built again once the app is active. The log then says "back early: the decoder came back in software".
- **Control Centre:** your log shows nothing switching under it, only one frame copied for PiP as it opens (about 17 ms). If it still stutters, please send a log of just that.
- **Please check:** pull down Notification Centre while a video plays, wait 2–3 s and let it go, a few times. The log line "coming forward: video output back now, before the app is active" shows the new path.

### No spinner for the player's own rebuilds
- The spinner no longer shows for the seek the player makes after rebuilding its picture: leaving the app, Notification Centre, PiP starting and ending. That seek reads from the buffer, not the network.
- Any other spinner waits 0.35 s before it shows, and goes at once. Your log has ten short seeks in one second while the phone turned.
- A real wait for the network still shows it.

### Picture in Picture: what's still there
- **Your log, PiP at 19:48:** the picture held still three times as PiP started (277, 180 and 236 ms), and about 0.3 s coming back.
- **Why:** with Metal, each PiP start and end rebuilds mpv's video output and decoder, and mpv then seeks to where you were. mpv's own code always seeks after a change of output, and won't refresh the video alone.
- The spinner is gone. These short holds remain.
- The way round them is a renderer that doesn't switch: **OpenGL (being tested)**, Settings → Player → Renderer, added in 1.20.0. Please try PiP on it.

### Library: the fourth tile in the first row
- Thumbnails is now rows of tiles, each its own row in Home's list, with equal columns.
- It was one grid inside one row of the list. That grid kept a layout of its own when the number of columns changed: 6 in landscape on your phone, 3 upright.
- I couldn't make the overlap happen in the simulator, before or after. This removes the likeliest cause rather than one I saw happen.
- **Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 26.5):**
  - Four titles in Thumbnails, List and Details, turned to landscape and back: every tile in its own place, 3 + 1 upright and 4 in a row in landscape.
  - The same after closing the player in landscape, from Continue Watching and from a title page.
  - A unit test checks the rows: three columns put a fourth title in a second row.
- **Please check:** close the player in landscape and look at Home. If it happens again, send a screenshot and the report.
