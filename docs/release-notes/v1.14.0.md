**Install:** download `Folio-1.14.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It replaces GlassPlayer (same app, so your library, history and settings stay).

### GlassPlayer is now Folio
*All your media, together.*
- New name on the Home Screen and in Files (On My iPhone → Folio)
- New leaf icon, which follows the Home Screen's Light, Dark and Tinted icon styles. Settings → General → App Icon has two more: **Monochrome** and **Alternative**
- New launch: the leaf, its page sliding in under it, then FOLIO and the tagline before Home

### Light and dark mode
- Settings → General → Appearance: **Automatic** (follows your iPhone), **Light** or **Dark**
- The palette is the brand's ink, graphite, taupe and cream. The player always stays dark
- Checked screen by screen in Light, Dark and Automatic in the simulator: text on the accent colour (selected chips, the Play button, badges) switches with it, switches use a taupe track in dark mode so the knob stays visible, and progress bars on posters are white on a dark strip so they show on any picture
- Settings, Network and sheets now sit on the page colour too, and title pages get a soft fade under the status bar once you scroll

### Posters open into their page
- Tapping a poster grows it into its title page, and Back shrinks it back into the poster (iOS 18 and later). In the library, Discover, search, catalogs and streaming-service pages

### Note
- iOS draws the very first launch frame itself in your iPhone's own appearance, so if Folio is set to the opposite of your iPhone, that one frame shows the other colour before the animation starts
