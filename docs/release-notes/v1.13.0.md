**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.13.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Fixed: opening a title in Discover crashed the app
- Cause: 1.12.0 loaded a title's details and its season list in parallel with an `async let`; on films the season task was never awaited, and the Swift runtime aborts the app when such a task is torn down. Your crash report and the CI simulator showed the identical stack
- Now plain tasks. **Checked:** the simulator test that crashed on opening a film now opens a film and a series from Discover, scrolls to the cast and links, with no crash

### Player panels
- **Black full-screen panel:** a delayed "opening finished" step ran after an outside tap had begun closing the same panel and presented it again mid-close; UIKit showed it full screen. Every transition now carries a number and a callback from an older one does nothing; a panel is never presented while it is still on screen or closing; one that comes up full screen anyway is closed at once
- **Taps that needed repeating, the pill "bobble":** your 15:13 recording and log show the tap right after a close never reaching the app (no log line) — iOS ignores touches while its own ~1.2 s glass close finishes, so the pill showed its press highlight and nothing opened. New default **Quick** transition: a panel opens in 0.24 s and closes in 0.16 s with no system animation in the way, so the next tap goes straight through. The system morph is still in Settings → Player → Menu animation
- **One tap outside closes**, anywhere — including where the pill was (as you asked on the 23rd; 1.12 had made pill spots switch panels)
- Panels closed by themselves ~1 s after opening when iOS accessibility was reading the screen (found in CI by logging who asked); fixed. VoiceOver's escape gesture closes a panel
- The app's animations now run at 120 Hz on ProMotion screens (it was never opted in, so everything ran at 60)

### Title pages
- **Play** sits under the description (library and Discover), centred
- The square box around Play is gone (it was the glow's gradient rectangle); the glow follows the pill
- **Tilt you can see:** up to 10° with real perspective, a highlight that slides opposite the tilt, and every ~4.5 s a gentle rock while a band of light sweeps across. It follows the phone more and re-levels when you change grip
- Chips wrap to a new line instead of squeezing "1080p" onto two

### Library
- Tiles show the show's portrait poster (3 per row), not an episode still. Settings → Library → Tile artwork: Poster / Episode still / Last frame
- **Continue Watching resumes with the show queued:** library episodes, the WebDAV folder, or the rest of the Discover season (Discover entries from this version on). WebDAV search results queue their folder too

### Skip & Up Next
- The Skip pill is named after the file's chapter when it has a real name ("Skip Opening", "Skip Next Episode Preview"); otherwise by what it is and where it sits: Opening/Ending for anime, Intro/Credits otherwise
- Classroom of the Elite S1E1: IntroDB reports "intro" and "outro" over the same end credits (the opening song plays over them), so the pill said "Skip Intro". Overlapping segments are merged and an "intro" in the last part of an episode is treated as the ending → one **Skip Ending**. Checked in CI with the IntroDB answers from your log
- **Up Next** takes over at the credits (or a closing preview) and the Skip pill is hidden while the card is up

### Player
- **Stay in landscape** (Settings → Playback): starts in landscape and stays there when you turn the phone upright
- **Back to the player from PiP:** opening the app while PiP plays ends PiP and the video carries on in the player (Settings → Picture in Picture; on by default)
- Brightness and volume swipes only work from the outer 35% of each side; a vertical swipe near the middle does nothing
