### A hot phone after Notification Centre
- **What happened (9 Nov report, Dune on Metal):** before Notification Centre the film played on the hardware decoder at about 14% CPU; after it, at 110–155% for the rest of the film. iOS stops a background app's hardware decoder. With the sound playing on in the background, mpv kept decoding video, failed three frames in a row, and fell back down its list: to copy decoding, then, on the next trip away, to software, where it stayed. Decoding 4K HEVC on the processor is what heated the phone, not Metal's drawing (4–5 ms of GPU a frame). The 1.27.1 OpenGL report has the same drop to copy decoding after a trip away.
- **Now, back on screen:** if the decoder is no longer the hardware one it was, Folio builds the hardware decoder again, from the buffer: a short restart, nothing read again.
- **While away, a file on the phone:** if the decoder leaves the hardware, video decoding stops right away (the sound plays on), instead of grinding 4K on the processor for nothing; it's read again at once when you're back. A stream keeps its buffer and stops decoding video after 15 s, as before.
- OpenGL's 10-bit copy decoding is no longer marked as failed for a codec because of a drop in the background.
