**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.30.4.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.30.3. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

Your seven reports from 14 Nov, and a second, deeper code check done by following what you do through the app rather than file by file. It found about a hundred more problems; the ones that matter are below.

### Your reports
- **Up Next no longer skips an episode.** Its countdown counted by where the video was, so a seek into the credits finished it at once (0.17 s after the card came up on 14 Nov), and the seek you were making landed on the next episode near its end — whose own Up Next then moved on again. The countdown now runs ten real seconds from when the card shows, stops while you scrub, pause or have a panel over it, and a seek or end of file from one episode can no longer reach the next.
- **A slow stream reconnects by itself.** On 14 Nov one Real-Debrid connection fell to 1–2 MB/s for a file that needs 3, and the player waited every few seconds for over a minute; reopening it played at once. After three waits (or 15 s of waiting) within 45 s while reading slower than the file needs, Folio reloads the stream from a fresh link at the same place ("Reconnecting…"), at most once every two minutes.
- **Continue Watching offers the next episode.** Only episodes you could still resume ever had a card, and you'd closed each one in its credits, so the show disappeared. A finished episode's card now becomes the next one, from the same library, server or addon; a show you watched from an addon and from Real-Debrid is one card; closing in the credits counts as watched.
- **Battery.** On 14 Nov the phone used about 14% an hour with the app's own work at under a fifth of one core, so the cost was the radio, the screen and the GPU. Now:
  - the stream is read in bursts (and with more memory to spare, a bigger buffer), so the 5G or Wi-Fi radio can rest between reads instead of receiving all film long;
  - a video shown at up to 1.25× (Balanced) or 1.5× (Efficient) its size is drawn at its own size and scaled by the screen, one GPU pass instead of four;
  - the AirPlay button is a plain button: the system's picker, which kept animating and looking for devices, is made only when you tap it;
  - Home doesn't re-list Real-Debrid each time you come back to the app while a video plays.
  The `[energy]` lines in the log now also say how the network was used ("data in 4 of 15 2-s slots" means bursts).
- **AirPlay:** see above. The empty card at the top of iOS's list is iOS's own for apps that don't play through AVPlayer; Control Center's Now Playing has the title and picture.
- **Skip Recap and Skip Intro on the last episode.** One intro service answered first and the other, which had the recap and intro, was cancelled two seconds later and its answer never used. Both answers are now waited for and merged, a late one is added when it comes, and a failed lookup says why in the log.

### Kids profile
- Pages left open in Search or Discover (a Real-Debrid folder, a Plex show, a stream list) are closed when the profile comes on; the server browsers and stream lists also refuse to show while it's on.
- The downloads pill is hidden while it's on (it opened every download, and sat over the passcode pad); Delete, Fix Match, Share and web links are hidden.
- Search forgets the grown-ups' query and results, and the kids' searches don't go into Recent.
- The tab bar is hidden over the Settings lock on every iOS version; on iOS 17 the Network tab stays and says it's not in the kids profile (the freeze fix of 1.30.3 for iOS 17).
- A passcode pad open when Folio goes to the background is closed properly; lockout messages say the real wait in minutes; a profile restored from a backup without its passcode is turned off rather than locking you out.

### Playback
- Play during the wait for a stream's link no longer plays the last episode underneath; pauses (AirPods out, a call) during that wait are kept.
- Quick ±10 s skips, a picked track, and a pending "Keep Watching" no longer carry into the next episode; Try Again can't be overtaken by an older link answer; a picked stream after an episode ended plays instead of opening paused.
- Playback resumes after a call or Siri if it was playing.

### Library, servers, search, Discover
- Playing from a big Plex, Jellyfin or WebDAV folder no longer freezes reading the login once per file.
- A film in a folder of different films is its own title: Play plays it alone, and "Delete all" can't take the folder's other films.
- Films on servers play alone; episodes queue in episode order whatever the folder's sort.
- Season queues leave out episodes that haven't aired.
- Fix Match updates Home at once; Home's watched marks and "Play next episode" count episodes watched from an addon, as the title page does.
- Pull to refresh on Home also refreshes the whole Real-Debrid library.
- One refused subfolder no longer says "Real-Debrid refused the login"; folders are re-checked in turn among the releases; libraries up to 5,000 folders.
- Home redraws only when the Real-Debrid library really changed.
- Search: a folder found by search opens the folder; films in a mixed folder are found by name; the keyboard's Search key works on iOS 17–25.
- Discover: "Find streams" waits for the title's IMDb id; a season that failed to load has Try Again; Airing now opens on the airing season; failed catalog rows retry; the watchlist keeps one entry per title.
- SMB folders list faster and don't time out by mistake; a server that's down says so instead of "You're offline".

### Downloads, sync, news, settings
- Downloads check free space before and while they run, say why they wait (Wi-Fi, Low Power Mode, charging), carry on by themselves after iOS stops them in the background, and resolve an addon's link once instead of for every part.
- Importing a settings file shows what it would add first; mpv scripts and config files in it are off unless you tick them.
- Anime plays waiting for AniList or MyAnimeList are kept while those can't be reached (they were dropped as "not found").
- A sync service whose sign-in was refused says "Sign in again"; switching a service off keeps its waiting plays.
- News from Folio: a second item shows after the first; tapping its notification opens it.
- Lowering "Delete watched downloads" asks first.

### Known and left

- The automatic reconnect, the burst reading, the frame size and the AirPlay button need your phone to judge: compare the `[energy]` lines and the battery over an hour of the same kind of file.
- The widget crash from 1.24.0–1.28.11 is still not identified.
