### Switching titles fast
- **A page that is going back can't play anything any more, and its touches stop at once.** Your 27 Oct log, 19:12:55: the page started going back ("page leaving: taps off"), and 114 ms later one of its episode rows played. 1.17.1's guard read "is the page leaving?" from a SwiftUI value, and that value is a copy from the page's last redraw, so the row's action could still see "no".
  - Now the page keeps that state in an object that the Back button sets *before* asking to go back and that iOS's "will disappear" sets. Every play action checks it at the moment it runs.
  - The page's view also stops taking touches right then, through UIKit's `isUserInteractionEnabled`, which applies to the very next touch. A tap meant for Home goes to Home.
  - If going back doesn't actually happen (a request made during another move can be dropped), the page takes touches again after 1.2 s.
- **Taps on a page that is still zooming in work again.** 1.17.1 ignored them, which is why your buttons "were not getting registered". A swipe across a tile or row still isn't counted as a tap; the log says so when it ignores one.
- **What I could and couldn't test:**
  - The simulator test can't tap fast enough to land inside the zoom: XCUITest's taps are about a second apart. It switches titles 8 times and passes on both 1.17.1 and 1.17.2 (nothing played, the other title opened every time).
  - An in-app probe that checks every frame where a touch would land while a page goes back found the same in both versions: from the first frame, Home.
  - So the simulator doesn't show what your phone showed. The fix targets the failure your log does show. The log records every ignored tap and why, so if this still happens, it will say.
- **Infuse** (notes in your Infuse repo): it opens title pages with UIKit's standard push, not a zoom. It never hides the bar and throttles taps to one per 300 ms, and its episode pickers select on the first tap and play on a second tap or the Play button. If you'd rather have Infuse's standard push than the zoom, it's a small change.

### Leaving the app (Notification Centre, Control Centre, Home Screen)
- **The buffer survives a pull-down of Notification Centre.** Pulling it down sends the app to the background for a moment. While playing, Folio used to switch the video track off at once, and switching it back on reads the whole buffer again from the network.
  - Now only the video output goes, the same swap PiP does, which keeps the track and its buffer. Coming back builds the output again from the buffer.
  - If you're still away after 15 s with sound playing on, the video track goes off as before, so background listening doesn't decode video.
  - **Simulator test** (away for 3 s while playing, then back): 16.3 s of buffer before leaving, 13.2 s while away, 12.1 s a second after coming back. The 1.17.1 way starts again from about nothing. The video output came back in 19 ms and continued from where the sound was. Closing the player straight after was clean too.
- **Fixed: software decoding after returning to a paused video** (a bug from 1.17.1). The decoder rebuild ran at the very moment the app became active. Your log shows it falling back to software decoding for the rest of the file, using far more CPU. It's gone: coming back now builds a new video output and a new hardware decoder, as above.
