**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.28.8.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.28.7. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Native where iOS has it
- **Settings search** is the system's search field (iOS 26: glass, in the bottom bar, with its own Cancel and keyboard handling). The tab bar steps aside on Settings' first page to make room and is back on Home. Before iOS 26 the row at the top stays, because there the system's field needs the navigation bar that Settings hides.
- **Empty search pages** ("Search What's Yours", "No Results for …") are the system's empty-state view, as everywhere else in Folio.
- **Haptics** in the Play button, Track, download buttons and folders, the player's panel pill and the wrong-passcode buzz use SwiftUI's own. The ones fired from UIKit gesture code and the player model stay as they were: SwiftUI's only attach to views.

### Player
- **Brightness** shows as a number in a pill, like the player's other notes, with a sun that grows and shrinks as you swipe. It was a small bar of its own.
- **Volume:** iOS's own indicator was already used, but a check 0.3 s into the first swipe compared the volume with where the swipe began, failed for no reason, switched the indicator off for the rest of the run and made the volume jump back. It compares with what was last asked for now.

### Battery (from the 11 Nov report)
- **Widgets:** their files (through the Keychain on this install) and pictures are written off the main thread; a launch no longer rewrites every file and reloads four kinds of widget when nothing changed; Pick for Tonight is written once per change, not twice.
- **Play button:** no longer tilts with the phone, so the motion sensor isn't running on title pages. It still tilts under your finger and shimmers.
- **Diagnostic traces** turn themselves off a day after they're turned on. Yours had all three on (Library, Network and PiP), which log every tile that scrolls past, sample the picture 120 times a second around each trip away and save screenshots.

### What the report shows about the 6–7% in 27 minutes
- The film was a 4K Dolby Vision stream from Real-Debrid (9.35 GB). Decoding was on the hardware decoder the whole time, the GPU took 3.8 ms a frame, nothing was dropped and the phone stayed cool.
- Folio's processor use was about 40% of one core, and **25–30% of that was the thread that downloads the stream**. On lower-bitrate streams in the earlier log the same thread used about 1%. The engine decrypts the HTTPS stream with its own library (GnuTLS) rather than iOS's, which is the likely cost at 25 Mbit/s. This is not fixed in this build: it needs the stream to be fetched through iOS's own networking.
- HDR at high brightness is the other large cost, and it's the same in any player.
