**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.2.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player
- **Menus no longer close by themselves** mid-playback: subtitles, audio, speed and ••• are native iOS menus that keep the controls on screen while open
- **Liquid Glass progress bar** with chapter markers. Choose ticks, dots, segments or hidden, and pick a colour, under ••• → Progress bar (or Settings → Playback)
- **Your own markers**: ••• → Chapters & markers → Add marker here. They show as yellow diamonds on the bar
- The chapter name appears in a glass bubble while scrubbing
- Smoother: high-frequency mpv updates are batched off the main thread, and per-frame log forwarding and icon shadows are gone

### Home
- New design: Continue Watching hero with the frame where you stopped, quick-action chips, an On This Device grid with thumbnails, source cards and Recently Played
- Files saved into GlassPlayer → Media with the Files app **appear immediately**, no relaunch needed

### Discover
- **What's popular on Netflix, Prime Video, HBO Max, Disney+, Apple TV+, Paramount+, Hulu, Peacock and Crunchyroll**: Trending Everywhere, Netflix Top 10 shows and movies, then a section per service
- Tap a service for its full Shows / Movies catalogue; reorder or hide services and set your region from the ☰ menu

### Fixes
- Open File now uses the system document picker directly and makes sure iCloud and other provider files are downloaded before playing. If iOS refuses access, you get a clear message instead of nothing
