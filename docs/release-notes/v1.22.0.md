**Install:** download `Folio-1.22.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.21.0 (same app ID, so your library, history and settings stay). Keep app extensions if your sideloading tool asks: the widgets and Live Activities are one.

Picture in Picture now starts and ends without a pause. The screen and PiP show one and the same picture, so nothing has to switch. New: Live Activities for what's playing and for downloads, two subtitles at once, per-show speed and opening skips, next episodes downloaded on Wi-Fi, watched downloads cleaned up, library filters, a Recently added row, "Pick something for me", a kids profile, Handoff and a What's new page. Nothing here has been tested on a phone yet.

### Picture in Picture without the pause
- **One picture for the screen and Picture in Picture, by default.** Each frame is drawn into the same picture the system's PiP shows, so starting PiP, coming back from it, pulling down Notification Centre and visiting the app switcher no longer pause the video or show a spinner. Before, the default renderer had to hand mpv's video over to a different output each time, and mpv rebuilt its picture and searched for its place again: the 0.2–0.3 s freeze.
- **The renderer is called "Standard" now** (Settings → mpv → Renderer), and everyone is moved to it once. "HDR (Metal)" is still there for real HDR and Dolby Vision brightness and compute shaders, with the short pause it has always had. Standard shows HDR at normal brightness.
- **Fixed on Standard:**
  - Fill and pinch-to-fill work.
  - Picture in Picture started after the app was already in the background shows the picture, not a still frame with sound.
  - A quick trip to the Home Screen or the app switcher no longer makes mpv reload the video. The video track is only turned off after 15 s away.
  - Subtitles placed below the picture stay on screen.
  - Each frame is drawn once, not again for every update that came in meanwhile.
  - Small videos on a big iPad screen aren't drawn larger than twice their size.

### Player
- **Subtitles in two languages at once:** "Second subtitles (top)" in the player's Tracks menu shows another subtitle track at the top of the picture.
- **Speed and the second subtitle are remembered per show,** with the audio and subtitle tracks, delays, Fill and Anime4K.
- **"Always skip openings" for one show,** in the player's Settings menu. It skips that show's openings by themselves, whatever the setting for every video is.

### Live Activities
- **What's playing** on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: title, episode, progress and time left, with play/pause and 10-second skip buttons.
- **Downloads** too: the file (or "3 files"), overall progress, speed and time left; tap to open Downloads. It ends with "Done" when the queue finishes.
- Turn them off in Settings → Downloads → Live Activities.

### Downloads that look after themselves
- **Download next episodes** (Settings → Downloads → Automatic: Off, Next 1, 2 or 3). When you've watched most of an episode from a WebDAV server, or one downloaded from it, the next ones in its folder download. This only happens on Wi-Fi, never on cellular, a hotspot or Low Data Mode. SMB, Jellyfin and Plex can't download yet.
- **Delete watched downloads** after 1 day or 7 days, with their subtitles. Only files Folio downloaded and you've finished are deleted. Turning it on also removes the ones finished longer ago than that.

### Library
- **Filter by watched, unwatched or in progress, genre and year,** next to the sort menu. The filters stay set; an empty result says why, with "Clear filters".
- **Recently added:** the titles you added last, in a row on Home (hidden on a phone in landscape and at the largest text sizes, where it would push your library off the screen).
- **Pick something for me** (the dice in the library header): a random unwatched film, newer ones more often, with "Pick again".

### Kids profile
- **Settings → Kids Profile:** turn it on with a 4-digit passcode (Face ID or Touch ID can unlock it too). While it's on:
  - Discover, search and your library show only titles rated for kids, up to the limits you choose (G, PG, PG-13; TV-Y to TV-PG). Unknown titles are hidden.
  - Settings, the Network tab and the addon menus are locked away.
  - Siri, widgets and links from other apps can't open or play anything.
- Leave it from the Kids badge on Home with the passcode. After 5 wrong tries the pad waits 30 seconds.

### Handoff and What's new
- **Handoff:** a title page or a video on your iPhone can be picked up on your iPad, and the other way round. A video continues where you were. Both devices need the same Apple ID, and the same sideloading team signature.
- **What's new** shows once after each update, and in Settings → Help & Diagnostics.

### Tested

  - On the new Standard renderer the trace shows leaving the app and coming back with no video reload and no seek. Before, the video was rebuilt both ways.
