### Notifications (Settings → Notifications, was Reminders)
Laid out like your 7 Nov mockup:
- **A check for each group**; tap a group for its kinds (a switch each) and its sound:
  - **Continue watching:** the 1.24 moments (paused, cliffhanger, film or show nearly done, Friday, late night, binge, a week away, twelve days).
  - **New episodes:** the shows you track. Same switch as Settings → Tracking.
  - **Trending now**, **Picks for you**, **Your library**, **Weekly briefing**, **Just for fun:** new, below.
  - **Downloads:** a download finished. Same switch as Settings → Downloads.
- **Personality:** Subtle (plain facts), Balanced (the 1.24 voice), Unhinged. Each kind has two to six lines and moves to the next each time, so the same line never comes twice in a row. Just for fun is off in Subtle.
- **Quiet hours:** 9 PM–8 AM, 10 PM–8 AM, 10 PM–9 AM (as before), 11 PM–9 AM, none, or your own.
- **At most:** 1 a day (as before) up to 5, two hours apart. New episodes and downloads don't count. Trending comes at most three times a week; picks, library and jokes twice.
- **When:** the ones without a moment of their own come half an hour before you usually start watching (from the last three weeks; 7 pm until Folio knows).
- **Sounds,** for each group: Default, None, Folio's own (seven made for it), Apple's alert tones (Tri-tone, Chime, Glass, Horn, Bell, Electronic, Bloom, Calypso and the rest), or a sound of yours from Files (cut to 29 seconds; iOS plays up to 30).
- **Pictures:** the title's poster or still is attached to each notification.
- **Buttons:** hold a notification for Play (when it's about something to play), Tomorrow (again in 24 hours) and Fewer Like This (turns that kind off). A kind you dismiss three times in a row without opening it rests for two weeks; its switch says so, with Resume.
- **Test** (on in this build): leave Folio and every notification that's switched on comes, one every 20 seconds: 46 kinds, about 15 minutes. Your own titles where Folio has the facts, a sample where it doesn't. Opening Folio stops the run; leaving again carries on where it stopped, each kind on its next line. New-episode notifications wait while Test is on. **Send the next one now** sends one in 5 seconds.
- **Sent:** what's coming and what came, with why ("Up 4,973 places to #13", "Test, a sample: nothing of yours fits this one yet"), and what you did with it.

**What the new groups say, and from what:**
- **Trending now**, from IMDb's Most Popular films and TV (each title's rank, places moved, rating, number of ratings, genres and year, as on the live charts): the #1 film and show, a big climber, straight into the top 10, a new film rated 8.5+, a show rated 9+, popular but rated under 6, an old title climbing, passing 100K ratings, and your most watched genre filling the charts. Titles you've watched are left out.
- **Picks for you,** from Discover's For you: a pick, a hidden gem rated 8+, and your genre this month.
- **Your library:** a film added 45+ days ago and never played, lots unwatched, a Top 250 or 8.3+ film you haven't watched, a file Folio found no title for, 25/50/100… films, and a film you own on the charts.
- **Weekly briefing:** Friday 5:30 pm, the week's top titles; new titles on the charts; on the 1st, the month's recap.
- **Just for fun:** opened Folio five times and watched nothing; a 20-minute visit with nothing played; days in a row; lots unfinished; crime documentaries; Friday movie night; Saturday's weekend forecast; a thriller before bed (if you're often up late); rain, cold or heat (needs a city); public holidays.

**What it can't do, and why:**
- **Folio has no server,** so the charts and the forecast are fetched when you use Folio, and in iOS's background refresh, which iOS runs when it chooses and not after you swipe Folio away (Apple's engineer, developer forums 685525). Trending notifications are only planned within four days of a fetch, and say "this week", not "today".
- **No "trending in your country":** IMDb's charts are worldwide, and TMDB's trending list has no country option.
- **Weather** is off until you set a city (Just for fun → Weather). Only its position, to two decimals, goes to Open-Meteo (free for non-commercial use, CC BY 4.0). Apple's WeatherKit needs a developer membership tied to the app. **Holidays** come from Nager.Date (free, no key), national ones only.
- **Apple's alert tones** are read from the iPhone's own system folder and copied for Folio when picked. That folder isn't a documented API, so the list shows only what your iPhone lets Folio read.

### Fixed (from a code audit, 7 Nov)
- **Lock Screen, Control Center and AirPods went back by the forward step:** with Forward 30 s and Backward 10 s, back jumped 30 s. It uses Backward skip now.
- **Your Week widget** kept the week's total until Folio was opened. It now drops a day at each midnight by itself (the counts say "Open Folio for the counts" until then).
- **Downloads widget:** after its estimate ran out, "3 min" counted up. It now says "finishing".
- **Continue Watching cards** could show the first card's menu on the second (the whole-row menu 1.24.1 fixed for tiles). Each card has its own now.
- **Now Playing** opened on Playlist for a stream whose chapters came after the player was ready. It opens on Chapters unless you picked a tab.
- **"Add marker at…"** kept the time the tab was opened at; it follows the video now.
- **Server folders and the Network tab** redrew every row on each download's progress tick, about 5 times a second. Each button redraws only for its own file now.
- **Pause all, Resume all, Cancel** started each queued file for a moment on the way (each pause freed a slot). They change everything first now, then start what should run.
- **The downloads dot** didn't pulse when downloads started after it had appeared grey.
- **Near the end of a streamed episode** Folio asked mpv every 2 seconds whether the file had been read to the end, waiting on the main thread. It asks off the main thread now.
- **History** is saved off the main thread when the player closes, one save at a time (two could run at once and an older list be written last).
- **Not done:** the frame grab for Continue Watching's picture when the player closes still runs on the main thread. The simulator can't grab frames, so it needs measuring on your phone first.
