**Install:** download `Folio-1.22.1.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.22.0 (same app ID, so your library, history and settings stay).

Metal is the default renderer again. Downloads are faster and no longer fail at the end after an update. The kids profile really locks. Also: the OpenGL renderer gets the HDR and 10-bit support it was missing, plus a crash fix and the rest of your 4 Nov list. Nothing here has been tested on a phone yet.

### Renderer
- **Metal is the default again,** and anyone 1.22.0 moved to OpenGL is moved back once. On 1.22.0's OpenGL some files didn't play, most of all 10-bit HEVC (most anime releases).
- **OpenGL now matches the design of the player you pointed me to.** That player draws with OpenGL ES into pixel buffers that are both the picture on screen and Picture in Picture's source, which is why its PiP never pauses. Folio's OpenGL renderer worked the same way, but two things were missing:
  - **10-bit files play.** mpv's iOS OpenGL ES code can't draw the hardware decoder's 10-bit frames and showed black. The decoder now hands OpenGL 8-bit frames.
  - **HDR is shown as HDR.** An HDR video is drawn in BT.2020 with the PQ or HLG curve into 16-bit frames, labelled with its colour and light levels, on an HDR layer, in the app and in PiP alike. HDR files decode in copy mode to keep all 10 bits.
  - **To try it:** Settings → mpv → Renderer → OpenGL, with "HDR output" on. Please test a 10-bit file, an HDR file and PiP on it. If it holds up on your phone, it can become the default.

### Downloads
- **No more "You don't have permission to save the file" at the end.** A download saved its destination as a full path, and an update moves the app's folder, so a download queued before an update tried to save into the old one. Destinations are now kept relative to Documents, and old saved ones are moved over.
- **Faster:** a big file from a server that allows it (Real-Debrid does) downloads in several parts at once, 4 by default ("Connections per file", 1–8, in the Downloads settings). On a test server limited per connection, 4 parts were 4 times as fast.
- **Fixed a crash** on "Remove finished" with "Delete files". It could also remove the wrong download and leave a file behind.
- **The Live Activity shows progress while Folio is in the background.** The ring, bar and time left move on their own from the measured speed, and are corrected whenever Folio runs.
- **A new look for both Live Activities:** glass panes and buttons, a water ring for downloads, and the picture's own colour glowing around now playing.
- Downloads now log their start, retries, failures and save path, so a report shows what went wrong.

### Kids profile
- **Every way out asks for the passcode or Face ID:** turning it off, changing the limits or the passcode, opening Settings and the Kids badge on Home. Before, "Turn off" worked without anything.
- **The passcode screen looks like the Lock Screen's:** circles, a round keypad with letters, Face ID first.
- **For you shows only titles rated within your limits.** Its titles carry no rating, so they were let through by genre (animation counted as kids). Now each one's rating is looked up, and unrated titles are hidden.

### Tracking
- **Times are your own,** checked with tests from Melbourne and New York (they already came from each episode's exact air time). "In 2 days" now counts days in your time zone.
- **A tracked show says so** on its library page: a "Tracking" button and the next episode's air time. This works for any series, and matches the Discover page by any of the show's names.
- **Episode tiles show "New" and "Airs Thu 9:00 PM",** on both title pages.

### Player
- **The controls sit 78 pt from each side in landscape** (they were 96 pt on an iPhone 17 Pro Max).
- **At the end of a file the player closes,** instead of showing Replay and Close.
- **Control Center:** Folio now asks for remote control events itself and keeps Now Playing's time up to date after a seek, with the saved frame as the picture. Couldn't be checked in the simulator.
- **Continue Watching keeps the newest season.** Playing an older season of a show from your library no longer replaces the newer one you were watching from Real-Debrid.

### Also
- **Title pages in landscape:** the back button and the buttons at the top no longer sit in the screen's corners.
- **Logs and reports hide Real-Debrid file links.**
