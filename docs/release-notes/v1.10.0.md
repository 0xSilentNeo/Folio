**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.10.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Panel buttons: taps that were delayed or lost
- Your recording shows a tap on Settings held for 0.3 s that did nothing, then a second tap that opened the panel. The player asked iOS to defer screen-edge gestures on every edge, and that setting is the one that holds back touches near the edges. It's off now, and the bar sits higher, clear of the home-indicator strip
- Panels now open from the tap itself (one frame sooner) and are sized before the animation starts, so they no longer flash or resize while morphing
- The morph itself: measured 0.25 s. The pill should glow and swell while the finger is down, which links it to the morph; it didn't on your phone. In the CI simulator it does glow, and the edge-gesture setting is the difference between the two
- In CI, 10 of 10 quick taps opened their panel, and a long press opens it on release

### Player
- Controls span 80% of the screen in landscape (Settings → Playback → Controls width, 60–100%)
- Lock button next to PiP (removed from the Settings panel)
- Panel titles no longer clipped at the top; panels stay clear of the top buttons
- Fixed: the Media info card, toast, error and Tune UI card were each drawn three times

### Up Next
- An Up Next card with the next episode's art appears when the credits start and counts down 10 s of playback (pauses with the video); Play Now or Watch Credits
- Credits time comes from the file's chapters, then IntroDB and TheIntroDB (community databases), then AniSkip for anime; without data, 30 s before the end
- No countdown when a scene is known to follow the credits (IntroDB post-credits, or more than 30 s of non-preview video after the ending)
- Anime databases are only used for anime: an explicit anime hint, animation per IMDb, or fansub-style names. Later seasons look up that season's entry and are skipped when no clear match exists (no more season 1 timings on season 2)
- The same sources now give live-action shows and films a Skip Intro / Skip Credits button

### Less heat
- New Picture setting (Settings panel and Advanced): **Efficient** (default) uses hardware-filtered scaling and no extra passes; Balanced is sharper; Enhanced is the old Enhanced Quality and uses the most power.
- The library window is hidden while the player is open, so it does no work during playback, and saving your position every 5 s no longer re-renders the library
- The hidden IMDb page loader unloads the page after each lookup
- The decoder in use (hardware or software) is now written to the log

### Library
- Artwork is cached on disk, decoded off the main thread and downsampled, so posters appear immediately after the first time (before, every poster was downloaded again on each launch)
- Now Playing: playlist rows show S01E02 · episode title, or the full file name (Settings → Playback → Playlist names)

### New settings
- General: Startup page, Hide spoilers (blurs art and hides the synopsis of episodes you haven't finished), Show IMDb ratings
- Library: Movies / TV Episodes / Other counts, Scan for changes, Refresh metadata, Metadata fetching on/off, Library cache size with a clear button
- Playback: separate forward and backward skip, swipe gestures on/off, Up Next on/off
- Subtitles & Audio: subtitles on/off by default, downmix volume (original/normalised), LFE in stereo downmix
