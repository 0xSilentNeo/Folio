**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.26.3.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.26.2. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Settings, closer to iOS's own
- **Each setting with an explanation is a group of its own, with the explanation under it**, as iOS Settings does (True Tone, Background App Refresh). The text starts below the whole row, switch included; it sat 2 pt under the switch inside the row. Rows that depend on a setting (Remembered Shows, Hold speed, Marker colour) are in its group.
- **A long explanation shows two lines, fading out under "more"; a tap anywhere on it opens the rest** (and closes it again), sliding open instead of jumping. The grey is iOS's footer grey (it was a shade lighter, about 1.7:1 on the cream).
- Two explanations that only repeated their setting's name are gone ("The tab the app opens on", "Shows the IMDb rating on title pages").
- **New tiles on the main list:** Folio's own earthy colours (clay, brick, ochre, sage, pine, slate, plum, graphite, sand) instead of iOS's rainbow, with a soft top light and a hairline edge; glyphs that say what's inside (the engine's chip, the library's books, Discover's own tab glyph), and About Folio is the Folio leaf. White glyphs are at least 4:1 on every colour.

### Notifications
- **Personality is a slider** from Subtle to Unhinged, over a notification worded in the tone it's on, so each stop shows what it changes.
- **Quiet Hours is one switch with your own From and To** (the five presets are gone). Off, it keeps 10 PM – 9 AM ready for when you turn it on.
- A group's page no longer has its own Sound row: Notifications → Sounds has every group's.

### Player
- **Chapter markers are segments by default** (they were ticks). A style you picked stays.

### Tested
- Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.5): the main list's tiles, Playback and Notifications pages, a description opening on a tap. Unit tests: 490 pass. UI tests: Notifications (slider and Quiet Hours) pass.
