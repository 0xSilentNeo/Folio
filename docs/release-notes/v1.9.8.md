### Panels: glass source for the popover morph
- The 1.9.7 trace from the device showed the popover's layer at full size and opacity from the first millisecond: iOS presented it with no transition (the close did fade). In Outplayer the popover is presented from a UIKit Liquid Glass button (`VideoMenuButton` on a `UIGlassEffect`); iOS 26 morphs a popover out of a glass source element and does nothing for an invisible anchor
- The Tracks · Settings · Now Playing pill is now real UIKit Liquid Glass: a `UIVisualEffectView` with `UIGlassEffect` (capsule) holding three `UIButton`s, and the popovers present from those buttons (`sourceItem`). Press feedback is UIKit spring scale. The animation trace stays in the log so the next report shows what changed

### Now Playing tabs
- The Playlist / Chapters / Markers thumb was two different views (glass while moving, pink when settled), so SwiftUI replaced it rather than sliding it. It is one view now: it slides with the finger, stretches slightly while dragged, and settles to the accent colour — the tab-bar behaviour
