**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.28.11.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.28.10. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### What your report shows about the new engine
- Same show, same quality (MobLand, 4K Dolby Vision from Real-Debrid): Folio's processor use fell from about 34% of a core to about 13%, and the stream's download thread from 20–25% to under 2%. The largest reading on the new engine was 36%, for a moment after opening the app.
- Cowboy Bebop (interlaced, decoded in software, a local file) is unchanged at about 36%, as expected: that cost is decoding, not the network.

### One search
- The search button now opens the same page from every tab: **your library and servers first, then Movies & Shows** from IMDb as you type, and your addons' catalogs after you press Search. Recent searches and the Clear pill are on it too.
- The text is no longer dropped when you open search from another tab.

### Player
- **AirPlay button** in the top row: iOS's own output picker. The sound goes to an AirPlay speaker or Apple TV; the picture goes there by Screen Mirroring, which the same sheet offers. (Folio's engine can't hand a video to an Apple TV to play by itself, as Apple's own player can.)

### Home Screen and Control Centre
- **Resume on the app icon:** long-press Folio's icon for Resume, with the name of what you watched last.
- **The Control Centre control:** tested in the simulator on an install signed as built, it shows its play icon. Your log says iOS did ask Folio to draw it on your phone, and it still came out blank: that points to the re-signed install, like the missing App Group, and not to anything left to change in the code. The app icon's Resume, the Lock Screen widget and a Shortcuts control that opens `folio://resume` all work without it.

### Discover
- **New keeps its genre:** choosing New showed only the year and dropped your genre. It has both now.

### Real-Debrid on Home
- **The count and size:** Real-Debrid changes a folder's contents without changing its date, so Folio only noticed when every folder came due for a re-read at 24 hours: the count ran short for a day (539 files), then jumped (635) with the same 220 folders, and that refresh took 30 seconds. A share of the folders is now re-read on every refresh, oldest first, so each is seen again within a few hours and no refresh reads them all.

### What's new
- After an update over several versions, the What's New page lists what each version since yours brought, not only the newest.

### Housekeeping
