**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.26.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.25.2. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### From your 8–9 Nov report
- **Picture in Picture stopped starting by itself.** Your logs: it started on the first file of a session, and failed every time after the player had gone on to the next episode (18:06, 19:12, 19:29, 19:42, 20:31, 20:44) or a menu had been open. Automatic PiP is now armed again as each file starts, as play resumes and as the app leaves, with AVKit given the current playback state. The setting changed in the player now applies at once too.
- **A portrait video (AV1, HLG HDR) went black with sound until the phone was turned.** Two causes:
  - The player turned to landscape for every video. It now follows the video's shape: a portrait video opens upright (a file on the phone is measured first; a stream turns back once its shape is known), and the next episode can turn it again. Not with Stay in landscape or rotation lock on.
  - With HDR, frames change from 8-bit to half-float at the same size, and the old frame description stayed: every frame was refused by the layer. A new description is made whenever a frame doesn't match it, and a refused frame is logged.
- **The poster changed a moment after opening a title from Discover.** The page now keeps the poster you tapped (a sharper copy of the same picture), and the picture it opened with stays until a better backdrop has loaded: no blank in between.
- **Landscape title pages:** the backdrop was cut through its middle, losing heads; it's now cut near its top, and the band is a little taller. The fade under the top buttons reaches both screen edges instead of a box that stayed at the top as you scrolled.
- **A new genre on Discover showed nothing until scrolled up.** Fixed, and the page goes back to the grid's heading when you change a filter. Picking the genre already shown no longer reloads it.

### New
- **Watchlist:** the bookmark at the top right of a title's page, or hold a poster in Discover. A row at the top of Discover and a page with all of them (series or films, newest or A–Z).
- **Airing now** on Discover: series with new episodes this week (TVmaze) or anime airing this season (AniList), switched with a pill. Off in Settings → Discover.
- **Sync** (Settings → Sync): Trakt gets films and episodes you finish (and your watchlist, with an import of Trakt's); AniList and MyAnimeList get anime progress, never backwards. Each needs your own free app's Client ID from that site (the page links to it); nothing is sent until you connect. What can't be sent waits and goes later.
