### Widgets: Clear in light mode, Tinted in dark mode
- **What you said:** the paper-and-ink widgets looked out of place in light mode and their colours were off. You wanted the Clear look in light mode and the Tinted look in dark mode, with artwork in colour; Clear and Tinted made the artwork and other parts see-through.
- **What iOS allows:** a widget is see-through only in iOS's own Clear and Tinted Home Screen looks. In the normal look, a clear widget background shows no wallpaper (Apple, "Displaying the right widget background"; a developer forums question about exactly this has no answer).
- **Now:**
  - **Light mode:** glass, drawn by Folio. The title's own artwork is softened behind a smoky veil and a light rim, with white text, like Clear over a wallpaper. A widget with no artwork uses a soft colour of its own.
  - **Dark mode:** the Tinted look. Deep blue glass with text in a light tint.
  - **iOS's Clear and Tinted:** artwork keeps its colours at full strength (Apple keeps full colour for media artwork there), and so do the shadows under text on a picture. Text and bars are near solid.
- **The poster shelf:** posters were stretched into landscape tiles with a gap under the first row (your Home Screen screenshot). Each poster now gets its size from the widget's.
- **Tested:** the lab draws every widget in every look in the simulator; checked one by one. **Needs your phone:** the real Home Screen in Light, Dark, Clear and Tinted.

### Downloads
- **Unfolding a group slid its files in from the top, and the list jumped** (your 10:45 recording, Classroom of the Elite's 25 files). The sheet is now a list that builds rows as they scroll in, and unfolding adds rows in place with a short animation.
- **Scrolling and swiping were sluggish:** every row redrew on every speed update, 16–19 a second. Rows now redraw only when their own file changes (in the simulator, 47 redraws in 10 s instead of 698).
- **Swipe to delete:** now iOS's own swipe actions. A finished file has a grey Remove and a red Delete, which asks first, and a full swipe works. A running file has Cancel.
- **Speed for each file:** a running file reads "74.5 MB of 1.52 GB · 8.4 MB/s · 8 connections", measured over five seconds so it doesn't flicker.
- **The speed card covered the last row**, with the row's name showing through it (your screenshot). On iOS 26 and later it sits in the system's bottom bar with a solid backing. Discover and Search now leave room under their last row while the pill shows; Home and Network will too.
- **Dynamic Island:** the downloads Live Activity is centred and reads "1.31 GB of 3.12 GB · 2.4 MB/s". When iOS 26 runs downloads in the background, iOS draws its own progress, and Folio can only set its text: it now ends with the speed. Its layout is iOS's.

### Trying the reminders
- **When they come:** never right after you close Folio. They're planned each time you leave Folio and taken back when you open it, at most one a day, never between 10 pm and 9 am:
  - after a late night (watched past midnight): 12:30 the same day;
  - a film more than half watched: the next day, 8 pm;
  - an episode finished with the next one in your library: the next day, 7 pm;
  - four or more episodes in a day: the next day, 6 pm;
  - an episode stopped mid-way: two days later, 7 pm;
  - two or three episodes left of a show: three days later, 7 pm;
  - Friday, 6 pm, for a show you're in the middle of;
  - a week away: 7 days after you last watched, 7 pm; the darker one at 12 days;
  - a new episode of a show you track: when it's out (the tracker's notification, in this voice between 9 am and 10 pm).
- **Quick timing for testing, on in this build** (Settings → Reminders): leave Folio and the reminders it would send over the next two weeks come 20 seconds later, then one a minute, with their real lines. Opening Folio takes back the rest; leaving again brings them again. Turn it off for their real days. Tested in the simulator: "Lab Show is paused at 6:50. Everyone's frozen mid-sentence." came 20 seconds after leaving Folio, then "It's Friday. Lab Show has 3 episodes left. Coincidence? No." a minute later.
- **New:** Settings → Reminders → **Send a test reminder** sends the next line Folio would send in 10 seconds, and says when the real one is due. Tested in the simulator: "Lab Show is paused at 6:50. Everyone's frozen mid-sentence." arrived 10 seconds after the tap.

### Slow and glitchy spots, from your 6 Nov report and a code audit
- **Play and pause froze the screen:** each tap waited for mpv, which restarts the sound output on unpause. Your report has a 359 ms freeze on Play after a minute paused, and 40–73 ms on others. Pause, play and the sound-off during quick skips no longer wait.
- **The player's Settings menu dropped 80 ms on every open:** it listed your shaders folder four times and decoded your saved commands on every redraw. Both are now read once.
- **Swiping and scrubbing redrew the whole player on every finger move:** the scrub time and the swipe HUD's value now redraw only the time bar and the HUD.
- **Series pages on Discover downloaded the same IMDb page twice:** in your report that took 2.5 s, then 1.5 s, before a page was ready. The first season now comes from the page already loaded. Checked in the simulator: "10 episodes, from the seasons page", ready in 2.9 s.
- **Real-Debrid posters flashed initials and faded in again** whenever a row was rebuilt. A poster found once now shows at once from memory.
- **Now Playing's list kept the old picture and title** on a row when the queue changed (rows were known by position). Rows are now known by their file, and show the cached picture in their first frame.
- **Diagnostics → Last log froze on open:** up to 400 000 characters were in one text, re-filtered on every redraw. The log now builds line by line as it scrolls, and the app log is read off the main thread.
- **The trace log (Library and images) wrote two lines per scrolled frame** for List and Details rows. It now logs a row's place once it settles.
- **Adding an add-on:** "Paste from clipboard" is now the system's Paste button. It shows no permission prompt, and nothing waits on one; a 1.6 s freeze on 3 Nov came from that prompt.
