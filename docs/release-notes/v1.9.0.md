**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.9.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Library
- **Series are merged**: episodes of one show become a single tile ("MobLand · 6 episodes · 2 seasons"), like the WebDAV browser script. The tile opens the show page with season chips; tap plays the next unwatched episode
- Home shows the **whole library** (no more "5 files + All"), with **Sort** by name / date added / size, ascending or descending, remembered everywhere (Home, All, show pages)
- **Settings → Playback → "Open title page on tap"** (on by default): off plays the file straight away; series tiles still open their page

### Player
- Panels pop from their button with a slower, bouncier spring (0.55 s) from 10 % size, close with a quick shrink
- Switching between panels no longer leaves the previous panel's text ghosted behind — each panel is its own view now (the overflow seen in the Now Playing recording)
- Panels stop below the close / PiP row instead of running under it
- **Media info ↔ Live stats** are one card: the content crossfades and the card's height morphs, no replacement
- Tapping the video closes the card
