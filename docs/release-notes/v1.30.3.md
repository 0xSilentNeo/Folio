**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.30.3.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.30.2. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

Fixes from your 13 Nov report and recording, a few additions, and another code check.

### Kids profile
- **Settings no longer freezes after turning the kids profile on.** Turning it on took the Network tab out of the tab bar, iOS rebuilt the bar under Settings, and Settings stopped answering: no scrolling, no rows, no Back, with the tab bar drawn over the Face ID screen. The tab is now hidden instead, and the Settings search field keeps one shape whether locked or not.
- **Going back after turning it on doesn't ask for Face ID.** The Settings you were already in stay open for that visit; turning it off, its ratings and its passcode still ask every time, and leaving Settings or Folio locks them.
- **The kids button on Home is round, like the ones beside it,** and FOLIO no longer breaks over two lines.

### Addons
- **Playing an episode from its row queues the rest of the season,** as Resume does. Only the big button did; a row played one episode and stopped.
- **The stream picker in the player loads on its own** instead of with the view, asks once more if nothing came, and says "No Streams" with Ask Again rather than staying blank. Your log has the addon answering three seconds after the picker opened while the picker showed nothing. The picker now logs what it asked and what came back, so if it still happens the next report says why.

### Real-Debrid on Home
- **Releases deleted on Real-Debrid leave Home's row.** The row only changed when the library index refreshed, at launch at most every 10 minutes; "See all" lists the server live, so the two disagreed. Home now checks the server's top folder (one request) when it appears and when Folio comes back, at most once a minute, and at once after you've been in "See all".

### Search
- **"Yours" is "Library".**
- **Movies and Shows find more.** Typing uses IMDb's quick suggestions, eight at most of every kind; "Mon" gave two shows. Choosing Movies or Shows, "Show all", or Search on the keyboard now also asks IMDb's title search for that kind (up to 50, most popular first).
- **No lag when you close the search.** Closing rebuilt the whole page in the same frames as the system's animation; it now waits until the field has gone. Rebuilding the library index's search list moved off the main thread, its progress no longer redraws the page, and Plex and Jellyfin aren't asked again when you come back to the page.
- A server's releases are counted and capped by release, not by file; an earlier query's Plex or Jellyfin results never show under a new one; an earlier query's titles go when IMDb can't be reached; a sideways swipe on the chips or posters doesn't close the keyboard; thumbnails are decoded at their size.

### New
- **News from Folio.** About twice a day Folio reads a short list on its GitHub page and tells you about a new version or an announcement, as a notification and a card on Home. Nothing about you is sent. Off in Settings → Notifications. The kids profile never shows it.
- **Diagnostics can stay on.** Settings → Help & Diagnostics → "Keep on until I turn them off", for a problem that only shows up now and then. Without it the traces still switch off after a day.
- **What's New** has this release in it.

### From the code check
- A stream picked after an episode ended by itself plays instead of opening paused.
- Enter, < and > go to mpv when your input.conf binds them; otherwise to Folio's queue, and < at the first episode does nothing.
- Leaving the app waits again for the Metal renderer to finish a frame it had started.
- The kids lockout can't come back after a restart.
- The volume check takes its starting point again after a pause; "Try again" after a stopped stream doesn't jump back to the stop once you've moved; the Streaming Services region field starts empty when you haven't set one.

### Known and left

- The empty stream picker's cause isn't proven; the new log lines will tell.
- IMDb's title search goes through the same web-view fallback as Discover when IMDb asks for a check; it wasn't seen answering from here.
- The widget crash, the player file split and the lab code in the release build are as in 1.30.2.
