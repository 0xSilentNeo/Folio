### Segment thumb in UIKit
- The Playlist / Chapters / Markers thumb (and Skip interval, and Movies/Series on Discover) is now a UIKit control: a Liquid Glass capsule thumb moved with UIView springs. Tap: the thumb springs across, glass while moving, accent colour once it lands. Drag: it follows the finger, slightly stretched, and snaps to the nearest segment on release — the main-tab-bar behaviour. SwiftUI position animations were not running inside the panel on the device, which is why the previous fix changed nothing
