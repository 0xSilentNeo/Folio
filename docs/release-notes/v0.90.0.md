**Test build.** Download `Folio-0.90.0.ipa` below and sideload it over 1.30.6. Back up your settings first: this is the first build with a lower version number than the one it replaces.

**The version numbers start again.** 1.x was the road here. From now it's 0.90.0, 0.90.1 and so on, and 1.0.0 when it's ready for everyone. What's New starts empty.

### Playing
- **The loading screen stays put.** Its background picture made the player taller than the screen, so the bar and buttons sat below it until the video started.
- **The loading screen is there when it should be.** At once for streams, for at least a second, until the first picture. A next episode that was got ready shows none.
- **Real-Debrid: another server is tried.** When a download server answers 503, Folio follows the link again, up to three times, and opens the file again by itself, up to twice. A deleted file or a refused login isn't retried.
- **Your place is kept when a stream drops.** A stream that ended just after it opened counted as watched and moved on to the next episode. Now it's "the stream stopped", at the place you were.
- **The error says what happened.** "One of the server's download hosts didn't answer. Trying again usually works."

### Airing
- The title sits at the top beside the poster.
- **Swipe down anywhere to close the sheet.** Pull to refresh was in the way; there's a Refresh button now.
- **Open Title Page.** The show's page on the right season, the episode that just aired highlighted.

### Continue Watching
- **Go to Show / Go to Title** when you hold a tile. The page opens on that episode.

### Notifications
- **Spread over the week and the day.** They went out on the first day and left the next days empty.
- The weekly limits follow your daily number.
- Nothing between 22:30 and 08:00, with quiet hours off too.
- Settings says "Up to N a Day" and shows what's planned for today and tomorrow.

### Under the hood
- Real-Debrid folders are listed again a few at a time, not a hundred at once after a day away.
- The diagnostic traces cost less, and the per-tile ones pause while the screen is recorded.
- The old "1.30.x is out" banners are gone.

### Tested
- **Unit tests pass:** 899.
- **Simulator UI tests pass:** 30, with new ones for the loading screen and Go to Show.
- **Not checked on a phone:**
  - the retries against Real-Debrid;
  - closing the Airing sheet with a real finger;
  - installing over 1.30.6.

### Known and left
- **Memory after a next episode was got ready** can stay above a gigabyte. This build logs what's needed to find out why.
- **Lag while recording the screen** isn't proven fixed. Turning the three traces off is the test.
- **"Up to 5 a day"** needs five things worth saying. Most days have one or two.
- **Open Title Page** is hidden for anime tracked without an IMDb id.
