### The hidden page that played things
- **You were right: a page you'd backed out of stays on screen, invisible, and takes your taps.** This happens when you go back before the page has finished opening: you open a title and swipe back while it's still zooming in.
  - The page flies back into its tile, and Home is fully visible about 0.35 s after you let go.
  - For about 0.8 s more, the real page is still the top page, full size and invisible, and every tap goes to it. A tap on another title did nothing. A tap where the page's episode row sits played that episode.
  - iOS only tells the page it's leaving when that second is over, so 1.17.2's protection didn't switch on in time.
- **Fixed:** the moment you let go and iOS says the page is going back rather than opening, the page stops taking taps, until it's gone. Your taps go to Home, which is what you see.
- **Tested in the simulator** (iOS 27). I recorded the screen and timed the taps to the millisecond, using XCTest's own event synthesizer; XCUITest's normal taps can't land this fast:
  - **Back before the page is open, then a tap where its episode row was:**
    - 1.17.2: 3 of 3 played.
    - 1.17.3: 6 of 6 reached Home, in two runs.
    - In a third run, one tap played, but not this way: the simulator froze for 1.6 s and then delivered the swipe and the tap together, so the swipe never counted as a swipe and the page never went back.
  - **Back before the page is open, then a tap on another title:**
    - 1.17.2: 4 of 8 opened it; the other 4 were lost on the hidden page.
    - 1.17.3: 7 of 8 opened it. The eighth was hit by the same kind of freeze.
  - **Back from a fully open page** (‹ or an edge swipe), then a tap on another title 0.24 to 0.8 s later: already worked on 1.17.2 and still does, 20 of 20.
  - **Taps on a page that's opening:** the recording shows a title page looks open about 0.15 s into its zoom, so taps from then on hit a page you can see. They still work, as in 1.17.2, rather than being ignored as in 1.17.1.
  - **All the earlier UI tests** for menus, title pages, Settings and Downloads pass. The menu-switch test missed once on its first run, while the CI machine was freezing for seconds at a time, then passed 8 of 8.
- **A title page also can't start playback unless it's the top page.** In the simulator, iOS makes Home the top page 10–20 ms before telling the leaving page. A tap's action now checks this at the moment it runs.
- **The log now shows where touches go while a page goes back** ("touch … ms after going back began → Home") and how long the page took to go. If this ever happens again, the log will show it.

### Going back from a fully open page (what 1.17.2 fixed)
- On 1.17.1, a tap in the first ~0.1 s after going back could reach the leaving page. The simulator shows this too: a tap where an episode row was reached the row's play action. 1.17.1's check stopped it in the simulator but not on your phone (your 27 Oct log, 19:12:55). 1.17.2 stops the page taking touches the moment it starts going back.
