### The hidden page that played things
- **You were right: a page you'd backed out of stays on screen, invisible, and takes your taps.** This happens when you go back before the page has finished opening: you open a title and swipe back while it's still zooming in.
  - The page flies back into its tile, and Home is fully visible about 0.35 s after you let go.
  - For about 0.8 s more, the real page is still the top page, full size and invisible, and every tap goes to it. A tap on another title did nothing. A tap where the page's episode row sits played that episode.
  - iOS only tells the page it's leaving when that second is over, so 1.17.2's protection didn't switch on in time.
- **Fixed:** the moment you let go and iOS says the page is going back rather than opening, the page stops taking taps, until it's gone. Your taps go to Home, which is what you see.
- **Tested in the simulator** (iOS 27). I recorded the screen and timed the taps to the millisecond (XCTest's own event synthesizer; XCUITest's normal taps can't land this fast):
  - Back before open, then a tap where the episode row was: on 1.17.2, **3 of 3 played**. Now 0 of 3; the tap reaches Home.
  - Back before open, then a tap on another title: on 1.17.2, 2 of 4 were lost. Now 4 of 4 open it.
  - Back from a page that's fully open, with ‹ or an edge swipe, then a tap on another title 0.24–0.8 s later: worked on 1.17.2 and still does (10 of 10). RESULT_A
  - The lab's recording shows a title page looks open about 0.15 s into its zoom. Taps from then on are on a page you can see, so they work, as 1.17.2 made them. They aren't blocked again as in 1.17.1.
- **A title page also can't start playback unless it's the top page.** In the simulator, iOS makes Home the top page 10–20 ms before telling the leaving page. A tap's action now checks this at the moment it runs.
- **The log now shows where touches go while a page goes back** ("touch … ms after going back began → Home") and how long the page took to go. If this ever happens again, the log will show it.
