### Long watching costs less
From your 7–9 Nov logs: a 1080p film idled at ~6% CPU, but HDR on the OpenGL renderer ran ~50% (4K), and an AV1 HDR video went to software decoding for the whole file (~200%).
- **HDR on OpenGL with Picture → Efficient** (your setting, "Coolest, longest battery") now draws straight from the video chip's frames instead of copying every frame out at 10 bits. HDR brightness stays; gradients have 8-bit steps. Balanced and Enhanced still copy for smoother HDR, except when the phone is hot.
- **No more software fallback:** if switching to copy decoding sends a file to software decoding (AV1 did), Folio goes back to the video chip within a second, and doesn't copy that format again in the player.
- **History is written every 30 s while a video plays**, not rewritten whole after every 5 s position save (~1,400 times in a two-hour film). Leaving the app or closing the player still writes it at once.
- Handoff is updated every 30 s (was every 5 s), PiP's frame path no longer reads a setting on every frame, and a title page's animation timer can no longer run on forever after its page is gone.
- Fixed a slowdown 1.26.0 brought in: past 500 history entries, the whole history was sorted every 5 s during playback.

### Settings, redesigned
From a design review against Apple's guidelines and your screenshots:
- **The main list has 5 sections instead of 8**, and every row opens a page (four sections held a single row, and "New episodes" was the only inline control). Rows have filled icons that grow with your text size; Kids Profile shows On or Off; Tracked Shows its count; the version moved into **About Folio**.
- **About Folio:** version, What's New, where the mpv folder is, the source code, and the engine and data Folio is built on (mpv, FFmpeg, libplacebo, libass, MoltenVK, Anime4K, TVmaze, AniList and more), which had no home.
- **Short explanations:** a long one shows two lines and More, only when it's really cut (Playback was a wall of text). The descriptions of the renderer and Picture are up to date (OpenGL has had HDR since 1.22.1).
- **Buttons look like buttons:** in a warm clay colour, where they were ink and read as plain text.
- **Asked first:** turning on Delete watched downloads (it deletes finished ones at once), Restore default commands, loading settings from a file (it installs its scripts), disconnecting a sync service.
- **Moved:** "New episodes" to Tracked Shows ("Notify me"); Clear Watch History and the artwork cache to Library; Zoom between tabs and Native menu morph to Help → Experimental; Presets is now Import & Export.
- **Notifications:** each group's row says On or Off and opens its page (a small checkmark sat inside the row).
- **Sync:** one row per service with where it stands, its setup on its own page; fields keep their names once filled.
- Settings pages keep a readable width on iPad; text fields, script switches, log text and icon buttons work with VoiceOver and larger text; the marker colour "Pink" is pink in the player; Library's counts are worked out once per redraw (every file name was parsed three times).
- Settings pages open by value on Home's stack, and the kids lock closes Settings and every page opened from it.
