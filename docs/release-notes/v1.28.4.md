**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.28.4.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.28.3. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Play and pause
- **The symbol changes at once.** The play/pause button morphed one symbol into the other (SF Symbols' replace effect), so the new symbol took a moment to settle even though the tap had already done its work.
- A trace that measured the screen for a second after every player button (a 120 Hz display link and a log line) now only runs with a diagnostics trace on.
- **Timed in the log:** each play says how long mpv took to answer and how long until the picture moved ("play: mpv answered in … ms, picture moving … ms after the tap"); each pause, mpv's answer. Your next report will show what's left, if anything.

### Widgets: frosted glass
- **What 1.28.2 did wrong:** iOS never lets a widget in the normal look see the wallpaper, so 1.28.2 made the glass out of the widget's first picture at full colour, and Dragon Ball's poster tinted the widget pink.
- **Now (your choice, 10 Nov):** the widget's own picture heavily blurred, with most of its colour taken out and darkened as far as white text needs, as frosted glass: the way Apple's Music widgets do it, with nothing to set up. Light from the top, a slightly darker edge and the bright Liquid Glass rim on top. Downloads and Your week, which have no picture, are neutral glass.
- iOS's own Clear and Tinted looks are as before.

### Airing Day widget
- **Sharper:** tracked shows keep TVmaze's medium poster (210×295) or AniList's large cover; the widget now fetches each service's full-size poster and keeps it at 600 pixels (it was 240, stretched three times over the widget).
- **Fits:** the poster's top shows (the faces), not its middle with its own title under the widget's ("MOBLAND" behind "MobLand").

### Control Centre
- **Resume:** an empty circle that did nothing. Its button now uses iOS's own "open a link" action with folio://resume, which Folio plays from (what you watched last, from its place), rather than Folio's own intent, which iOS evidently never matched to the app on this install: your log never saw a tap. Shortcuts and the Action button keep Folio's intent.

### Home
- **Watchlist button** at the top of Home, as on Discover.
