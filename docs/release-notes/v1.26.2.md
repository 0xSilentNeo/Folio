**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.26.2.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.26.1. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Watchlist
- **A bookmark at the top right of Discover** opens the watchlist in a sheet of its own (it was a row at the top of Discover). Titles open inside the sheet and play from there; Done closes it. The bookmark is filled once something is saved.
- All, Series and Movies each say when they're empty ("No Movies Saved"), and another filter or sort starts the list from its top.

### New lists start at the top
- **Discover:** another kind (Movies, Series), sort, genre or year takes you to the grid's heading, where the new list starts, wherever you were on the page. Until now it did that only when the heading had scrolled out of sight, and the emptied grid left too little page under the heading to get there: the new titles loaded under where you were. The grid now keeps a screen's worth of room, with placeholder tiles while a list loads.
- **Airing now:** Series and Anime each start from their first title (the other kind's row stayed scrolled where this one was).

### Now Playing opens on what's playing
- Playlist and Chapters open centred on the episode and chapter playing now, every time. Your 8 Nov log showed why they didn't: the panels are built ahead of time and kept, so the list moved to the playing row once, when it was built, and not when you opened it (the panel built on E22 at 19:40:41 opened at 19:40:51 with nothing in the log). It now moves on every opening, from the system popover and from the glass panels, and the log says where ("now playing opens on q21").

### Streams show their whole description
- **Sideways (and on iPad)** a stream's description shows every line; held upright it shows six, as before, and **a long press shows all of it** above Play and Download. Ellipses in the middle of a line ("Edgerunner…", "stremthru-cor…") are in the addon's own text.
