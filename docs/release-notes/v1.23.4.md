### Subtitles cut off when the picture is zoomed
- **What your 6 Nov recording and report show:** on OpenGL, in Fill, a two-line subtitle lost its second line below the screen ("And how do you two" without "fit into this aristocratic jigsaw puzzle?"). While pinching, the subtitles grew with the picture and were cut too.
- **Why:** the OpenGL renderer draws frames in the video's own shape, so PiP's window gets that shape. mpv's log: "Window size: 2346x1320" for a 16:9 file, and the same framing in Fit and in Fill. Fill is the display layer cropping the finished frame, and the subtitles are part of it. A pinch scales the whole picture, subtitles included, on both renderers.
- **Now, Automatic (the default):**
  - Subtitles keep their place and size on what's visible.
  - In Fill on a 16:9 file they sit 9% higher in the frame and at 82% of the size, which is where and how big they look in Fit. The log says what was set: "subtitles kept on screen: picture 1.22× fitted, 82% of its height on screen (OpenGL Fill) → sub-pos 91, sub-scale 0.82".
  - They follow a pinch as it happens.
  - On Metal, mpv does Fill itself and already keeps text subtitles on the screen. There Automatic only follows the pinch, which mpv now frames as your fingers move.
- **Stay as set:** subtitles stay where Position puts them on the video. Choose it in the player's Subtitles menu (When zoomed) or in Settings → Audio & Subtitles.

### The pinch
- **What the recording shows:** when the fingers lifted, one frame had the picture at the wrong size, then it settled.
- **Why:** the pinch scaled the view as a stand-in, then asked mpv for Fill and removed the stand-in 50 ms later, whatever frame was showing by then.
- **Now:**
  - **OpenGL:** Fill (the layer's) and the stand-in's removal happen in the same screen update, with no animation in between.
  - **Metal:** the pinch frames the picture in mpv as your fingers move (between Fit and Fill), so there's nothing to hand over.
  - Spreading your fingers past Fill or Fit stops 10% beyond them. In the simulator it went to 2.4×.
- **Tested in the simulator (OpenGL):**
  - Pinches between Fit and Fill.
  - mpv's log shows the subtitle values follow the pinch and end on Fit's or Fill's exactly. The first version counted the zoom twice for one moment, which this build fixes.
  - The simulator can't capture OpenGL video in screenshots, and Metal doesn't run there. **Needs your phone:** how it looks, on both renderers.

### Add-ons
- **What the reports show:** the saved add-on list is empty in your 5 Nov and 6 Nov reports. From 1.20.3 on, the logs show no add-on of yours in use, so it was already gone then.
- **The likely cause:**
  - 1.20.2 changed Folio's bundle ID, which made it a new app with fresh settings.
  - Add-ons carried over only by loading a settings file saved from the old app with "Include passwords and keys".
  - If the old app is still on your phone, that file can still be made there.
- **Also fixed, so it can't happen silently:**
  - If the saved list ever couldn't be read whole (one entry from a newer or older version), every add-on was dropped. The next change then saved the empty list over yours.
  - Now each add-on is read on its own, and an unreadable list is kept aside.
  - Each launch logs "[addons] N installed".
