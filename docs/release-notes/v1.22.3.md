**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.22.3.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.22.2 (same app ID, so your library, history and settings stay). It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone. A report after a download helps (Settings → Help & Diagnostics): its log says whether iOS showed its own progress for Folio.

### Downloads
- **iOS's own download progress** (iOS 26 and later). iOS shows it in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen: Folio's icon, "Downloading “Name”", "102.9 MB of 137.1 MB", and a ring whose button stops the download (as a pause, resumable). A checkmark shows when the queue is done. Folio's own Live Activity stays off meanwhile. The Dynamic Island is black for every app, so the ring is iOS's, not glass.
- **A faster engine,** downloading in Folio itself while iOS shows that progress:
  - Up to 8 connections per file (the new default; Settings → Downloads → Connections per file).
  - A free connection takes over the slowest range that's left, and a connection that crawls is restarted.
  - The file is written straight into place, with no joining at the end.
  - In the simulator, on a server allowing 2.5 MB/s per connection: 1 connection gave 2.5 MB/s, 4 gave 10 and 8 gave 19.9. On a server where every third connection crawled, 4 connections took 26 s against 419 s in 1.22.2.
- **If iOS won't show its progress for Folio** (before iOS 26, or it refuses), the log says so. New files then download in iOS's background session as in 1.22.2, in parts, so they carry on after you leave Folio, with Folio's Live Activity.
- **Time left in the Live Activity** now comes from the speed over the last 5 seconds, once 3 seconds of data have arrived. In 1.22.1 it came from the first readings, so it said "48:34 left" for a file that finished in 25 seconds.
- **Titles in Downloads fold away:** tap a title to hide its files and tap again to show them. Folio remembers which are folded.
- Downloads begun before this update finish the way they began.

### Home
- **Recently added is gone.** Its tiles were the library again, which sorts by date added too.
- **The round buttons beside Library** (filter, sort, view), Home's + and Discover's ⋯ open their menu out of a circle. They used to turn into a rounded square for a moment first (a simulator recording showed it frame by frame).

- Real-Debrid's speed on your Wi-Fi, its stop button, and the checkmark at the end.

  - All 364 unit tests pass, 36 of them new: the engine against a stand-in server checked byte for byte (several connections, pause and resume, no byte ranges, a dropped connection, a crawling one taken over, a web page instead of the file, a 404), the range splitting, the speed estimate (with the 4 Nov numbers), the progress texts and the folding.
