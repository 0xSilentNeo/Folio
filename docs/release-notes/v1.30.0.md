**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.30.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.29.0. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

Three more of the audit's bigger features in one build, and a code check of them with its fixes.

### Intros found on the phone
- **For episodes in your library (Media and downloads) that no source has an intro for**, Folio decodes the first ten minutes of the episode's sound and of a neighbouring episode's, and looks for the stretch they share. That stretch is the intro: the Skip pill, skipping automatically and "Hide intros I watch" treat it like any other.
- **When it runs:** after the episode has played 20 seconds, only while it's playing, not in Low Power Mode, not when the phone is warmer than "fair", and never with the kids profile on. One file at a time, at low priority. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **The first time, the intro has usually passed** by the time it's found; it's there from the next play, and the neighbouring episode has its own at once.
- **Settings → Skip → Find intros on this iPhone** turns it off.
- Not for streams or files on a server: only files on the phone.

### One watched state, one title page
- **An episode or film watched from the library is watched in Discover, and the other way round.** Marks made on either page show on both.
- **A half-watched episode resumes from either side**: the stream starts where the file stopped, and the file where the stream did.
- **Discover's title page has the library page's ⋯ menu**: Play, Mark as watched / unwatched, the season's marks, Share. Episode rows show the same watched and in-progress marks.
- Linked by IMDb id, season and episode. Extras, specials and titles without an IMDb match stay separate.

### iPad
- **Bigger posters and wider margins**: five across on an 11-inch iPad upright and seven on its side (it was six and nine small ones). Discover's grid, the 16:9 tiles and Files follow.
- **Readable columns** on both title pages, search results and downloads, with the hero picture still full width.
- **The tab bar can turn into a sidebar** with the button at its leading end (iPadOS 18 and later).
- **Pointer highlights** on buttons, tiles and cards.
- **Keys in the player**: Space play/pause, ←/→ seek by your steps, ↑/↓ volume, F fill, N next, Esc closes what's on top, then the player. ⌘F opens search. Hold ⌘ to see them.
- A narrow window (Slide Over, Split View) keeps the iPhone layout. The iPhone itself is unchanged.
- Not in this build: multiple windows, and Discover's horizontal rows keep their poster size.

### From the code check
- **"Start over" and a carried resume place survive Try Again**, and no longer apply to a second stream picked from the same page.
- **Discover's "Resume from" says where the stream will really start.**
- **A device handing off an episode only marked watched here** opens its page instead of a player with nothing to play.
- **Fix Match keeps two films of one name apart** ("Dune (1984)" and "Dune (2021)" shared one match). A film fixed in 1.29.0 whose name has a year needs fixing once more.
- **The intro finder:** a file whose sound can't be read is no longer decoded again on every play; an intro found before shows at once instead of after the online lookups; a shared stretch ending in the file's second half is not taken as an intro; it never starts while paused, or with both the Skip button and chapter marks off; leftover temporary files are removed.
- **"Mark this season as watched"** leaves out episodes that aren't out yet, as the rows do.

### Known and left
- A wrong on-device intro (two episodes sharing some other music early on) stays until the feature is turned off: there's no "Not an intro" yet.
- Home's tiles still show the library's watched state only; the title pages have the combined one.
- Marking a season watched in Discover marks its downloaded files too, so "Delete watched downloads" will remove them, as it does when marked in the library.

### Tested
- Unit tests pass, with new ones for the matcher, the watched state and the iPad layout numbers. Simulator UI tests pass on iPhone.
- **Not checked on a phone or iPad:** intro finding with real episodes (the engine in the simulator differs from the phone's), iPad landscape, the sidebar, pointer and every keyboard key.
