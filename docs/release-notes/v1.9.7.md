**Install:** download `GlassPlayer-1.9.7.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Measuring instead of guessing
- Every panel open now writes an **animation trace** to the diagnostics log: the popover's presentation layers are sampled at display rate for 0.8 s and summarised as `ANIMATED (n distinct states)` or `SNAP`, with the size/scale/opacity trajectory. Open each panel once, then Settings → Advanced → Diagnostics → share the report; that line says exactly what the device did
- A CI "UI lab" builds the app for the iOS 26 simulator, runs a scripted session (play → every panel → media info → stats) and publishes the recording and logs. In that environment plain `UIView.animate` runs (traced: 29 distinct frames) while view-controller presentations complete in one frame — which is why the device trace matters

### Library

- Folder/library listing is recursive
