**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.28.2.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.28.1. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Picture in Picture with the video zoomed
- **What happened (10 Nov recording, Dune in Fill):** PiP's window took the screen's shape (2.17:1) instead of the film's 16:9, and showed the top of the frame: the top black bar in, the bottom bar and the subtitles cut off. Fill was the video layer's "aspect fill" framing, and PiP builds its window from that framing.
- **Now:** the video layer always frames the whole picture, and Fill scales the player's view of it. PiP gets the whole frame in a window of the film's own shape, as Outplayer does, whatever the zoom; subtitles move back to their place in the frame while PiP is up.

### Zooming
- Fit ↔ Fill is a Core Animation spring drawn by the system's render server, so a busy moment in the app can't make it stutter. A pinch moves the same scale: nothing is swapped when the fingers come down or lift, the spring carries on from where they left the picture at their speed, and a quick flick decides the direction.
- Subtitles follow the picture at every step of a pinch or a zoom, not only at the end.

### Subtitles when zoomed
- A wide film (2.39:1) filled on the screen loses its sides: long lines now wrap within what's on screen (they ran off both edges).
- Placement goes to mpv without waiting for it, so a step of the zoom never holds the screen.

### Play and pause
- **The button changes at the tap**, not when mpv answers.
- **Play is instant:** the audio output stays open while paused, playing silence, so playing on no longer waits for iOS to start it again (359 ms measured). Seeks keep it running too, which should make them quicker. It closes after a minute paused, and a few seconds after you leave the app paused, so it costs nothing in the background.
- History, Handoff, Now Playing and PiP's state are updated just after the button, not before it's drawn.
- **Taps on the video answer at once:** a single tap no longer waits a third of a second to see whether a second tap follows.

### Every other player button
- The same wait was behind more buttons: choosing an audio or subtitle track (a new audio track has mpv open its audio output again, with the app waiting), speed and hold-for-speed, subtitle and audio delay, the second subtitle, Fill, aspect, Spatial Audio and volume. All of them now change on screen at the tap and reach mpv without waiting for it; a subtitle file you add is read by mpv without holding the app. The pinch on Metal's own layer no longer waits on mpv every frame either.

### Closing the player
- **What happened (10 Nov report):** closing waited for the frame Continue Watching keeps: a 4K HDR frame drawn and read back from mpv with the app waiting, up to a second, before the player started to close.
- **Now:** the player goes at once (sound stops at the tap); the frame is read behind it, then mpv shuts down. The audio session is let go after mpv's output has closed, off the main thread.

### Share sheet
- **What happened:** "This install of Folio has no shared storage for its extensions". Your install's signing has no App Group (the widgets use the Keychain instead), so the extension had nowhere to copy a video to.
- **Now:** a video from Files is passed as a bookmark to the file where it is, through the Keychain, and Folio plays it from there. If iOS doesn't lend the file (Photos hands over a temporary copy), Folio opens Add files, with the video at the top of Recents. Links already went through the Keychain.

### Widgets
- One clear Liquid Glass look in light and dark mode; the dark blue style is gone. The glass is made of the widget's own artwork, frosted, with light through it from the top and a bright rim. Its veil is only as dark as the picture needs for white text. iOS's own Clear and Tinted looks are as before.

### Less work behind your back
- **Glancing away costs nothing:** pulling down Notification Centre or a quick look at the app switcher used to re-plan every notification (a dozen, with pictures, removed again on the way back), rewrite the widgets' files and have iOS wake the widget extension to redraw them, 50 times in the report. That work now waits 12 seconds away and isn't done if you're back first. Tracking alerts are no longer removed and added again on each return when nothing changed.
- The hang watchdog stops while Folio is away (it woke the app twice a second through background audio and PiP), and a title page's transition log runs only with the library trace on.
- **Home:** each tile worked out a show's next episode four times a redraw, each a walk through its history; it's now kept until the history changes. The filter menu's genres and years are no longer rebuilt from every title on each redraw, and coming back to Home no longer redraws it whole.
- Release names are parsed with patterns compiled once, not a dozen times per name (a large Real-Debrid library parsed thousands on the main thread), and the parse cache trims itself instead of emptying.
- The tilting play button on title pages no longer animates without end from a steady hand's tremor.
- Downloads: progress changes the list once per update instead of twice, and the floating pill redraws at most twice a second; its pause button still answers at the tap. Marking a whole show watched changes the history once instead of once per episode.
- IMDb answers kept in memory are capped, and dropped on a memory warning (they're on disk).

### From the log
- **The hardware decoder on screen:** back while paused, then play, mpv found the hardware decoder gone and fell to copy decoding for the rest of the film (13:31:24). Folio now builds it again whenever it drops on screen (twice in two minutes at most).
- The hang log no longer counts the time iOS kept the app frozen ("main thread stalled 22157.4 s").
