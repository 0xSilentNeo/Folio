**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.30.1.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.30.0. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

Fixes from a review of the whole code and from the 12 Nov report. No new features.

### Memory
- **Preparing the next episode no longer doubles the buffer.** To open the next episode early, the buffer limit is raised so the playing file is read to its end. The limit stayed raised, and the next file filled a second buffer just as large: the report has the app going from 703 MB to 2310 MB after "150 → 768 MB", and from 1625 MB to 2756 MB after "150 → 611 MB". The limit now goes back to your setting the moment the playing file is read to its end, so the next file buffers within it.

### Player
- **The next episode plays when the last one ended by itself**, also when it wasn't prepared ahead (Up Next off, a short file). mpv pauses at a file's end and stayed paused for the next.
- **Next tapped twice quickly** no longer leaves the third file paused.
- **A zoom of your own survives a turn of the phone.** `video-zoom` from mpv.conf, a key or a script went back to 0 at every rotation, HDR switch and return to the app.
- **Each HDR episode is shown with its own light levels.** MaxCLL and the mastering levels were read only when HDR came on, so every later HDR file in a session kept the first one's.
- **Efficient dithers.** Its 8-bit frames could show bands in the gradients of 10-bit videos (most anime). Dithering costs next to nothing.
- **iOS's volume indicator stays with Bluetooth headphones.** A check that the swipe had set the volume failed on AirPods' coarser steps ("wanted 0.66, got 0.75") and switched to the player's own bar for the rest of the run.

### Alerts, Discover, sync
- **"Comes out today" alerts fire in the Americas.** An episode known by its day only is announced at 09:00, but was dropped from the plan once its stand-in time (12:00 UTC, 07:00 in New York) had passed, so any replan between the two removed the alert.
- **Discover's genres work on TMDB in any language.** With the phone in another language the genre wasn't found and the whole popular list was shown instead; in the kids profile that list was then treated as Family. A genre TMDB can't be asked for now shows nothing from TMDB and goes on to the next source.
- **Plays waiting for Trakt, AniList or MyAnimeList survive an outage.** Six failed tries deleted them, a server error or a rate limit included. Those wait now; only a refusal of the play itself counts.
- **An addon catalog shows the genre you picked**, also when picked while another page was still loading.

### Library and downloads
- **Continue Watching no longer loses an episode** on a WebDAV server whose address was saved without "https://".
- **A title page shows the picture of the episode it names.** After moving on to the next episode it kept the last one's still.
- **Pause then Resume keeps what was downloaded** (iOS's background downloads): resumed before the pause had finished, a file began again from the start.
- **Downloads held for Low Power Mode end iOS's progress task** instead of leaving it running with nothing happening.

### Diagnostics
- **The energy lines in the log say the battery level, the screen's brightness and its HDR headroom**, so a report can tell what a session cost and whether the screen was the reason.

### Known and left
- The review's other findings are not in this build: opening a `.lua` file installs it without asking, WebDAV logins follow links to other hosts, and the efficiency items (the always-on mpv log, poster sizes, the metadata cache).
- The widget's crash seen from 1.24.0 to 1.28.11 isn't found yet: its record needs that build's symbols to read.

### Tested
- Unit tests pass (583), with new ones for the day-only alert in New York, which sync failures wait, and dithering in every picture mode.
- The app was run in the simulator on the OpenGL renderer: the lab clip played to its end and the player closed and was freed; the energy line carries the brightness.
- **Not checked on a phone:** everything that needs the Metal renderer or a stream (the simulator here can't start the Metal renderer): the buffer limit going back after "read to the end" and the memory it saves, the next episode playing after an end, HDR light levels per episode, the zoom kept across a turn, the volume indicator with AirPods, and the battery level in the log (the simulator has no battery). Downloads' pause and resume and the TMDB genre fix were not exercised either.
