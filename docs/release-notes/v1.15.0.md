**Install:** download `Folio-1.15.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player menus
- **Native menu morph** (Settings → Playback, on by default): the menus are the system popover again, morphing out of their button with Apple's own Liquid Glass animation. Turn it off to use Folio's own glass, which can be interrupted at any moment.
- With Folio's glass: tap another button while a menu is still closing and the shrinking glass turns straight into that menu
- Now Playing: the title and the Playlist / Chapters / Markers tabs stay put; only the list scrolls, and it opens on the playing episode

### Library
- Title pages highlight the episode you watched last, and Resume plays it. Coming back from the player, the page moves to the episode you just watched
- Continue Watching shows one card per show (and no longer doubles up after a reinstall)
- Each episode shows its file size at the bottom right
- A folder of different titles shows as one folder tile: four of its pictures in a 2 × 2 grid, its total size at the bottom right, and how many items it holds
- Fixed: tapping the next title right after closing one could start playing the closed one (the page was invisible but still took taps)
- Tap a description to expand it, not just "+ more"

### WebDAV
- **Downloads:** a download button on each video (a ring fills as it downloads; tap to pause or resume), "Download all" in the folder menu, a floating speed pill while anything downloads, a Downloads screen (filters, one card per folder, pause / resume / retry, play when done) and download settings (Settings → Downloads). Downloads continue in the background and go into your library. Matching subtitle files come along.
- Episode labels always show the number: "S2 · E1 Beast Titan". Files numbered past season 1 ("Show - 26") are matched to the right season. OADs, Junior High and Movies are labelled as their own ("OAD 5", "Movie 1 · Guren no Yumiya") and no longer borrow the main show's episode titles

### Discover
- Find streams (and each episode row) zoom open into the streams page, like Add files

### Settings
- Pull down to close Settings works every time (the list no longer bounces at the top and swallows the pull)
