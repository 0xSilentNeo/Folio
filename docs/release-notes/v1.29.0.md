**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.29.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.28.11. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

The first of the UX audit's bigger features, the new Welcome, and the Control Centre control removed.

### Fix Match
- **When the poster, title or episode names are wrong** (or Folio found none): open the title's page, tap **⋯ → Fix Match…**, search IMDb and pick the right one. The search starts with the name read from the file.
- **Every file of that title follows**: all the episodes of a show, every copy of a film. The choice is kept, and survives Refresh Metadata.
- **Use the Automatic Match** in the same sheet puts it back.
- After choosing, the page closes and the title comes up on Home under its new name and poster.

### An opening screen
- While a video opens (after the first half second) the player shows **its name, where it's coming from** ("Connecting to Real-Debrid…", or "Waiting for the stream's server…" while an addon's link is followed) over its own picture, dimmed.
- **After 12 seconds: Try Again and Close.** Before, it was a spinner on black for as long as the server took.

### Welcome
- Three short pages on a first install, swiped or stepped through, with Skip: what Folio is, where your videos come from (the three setup cards), and the player's gestures. It was one long scrolling sheet with the cards below the fold on a small phone.
- The button's text is readable in dark mode (it was white on light clay).

### Control Centre
- **The Resume control is removed**, with its two intents and its diagnostics. It drew as it should on an install signed as built, and blank on a re-signed one, which Folio can't change. Resume stays on the app icon's long-press menu, and `folio://resume` still works from a Shortcut.

### Still to come from the audit
- A parsed, filterable stream list; one title page for library and Discover; two-way Trakt/AniList sync; iPad layout; external display; on-device intro detection; localisation groundwork.

### Tested
- Unit tests pass, with a new one for Fix Match. Not checked on a phone: all three features. To see the Welcome again without reinstalling, it only shows on a first install.
