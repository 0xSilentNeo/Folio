**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.28.0.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.27.1. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Metal, as smooth as OpenGL
- **To try it:** Settings → Video Engine → Renderer → **Metal**. Your renderer isn't changed by the update.
- **The Metal renderer now draws into the player's video layer**, the one Picture in Picture uses, as OpenGL does. mpv's gpu-next output (libplacebo) draws each frame into a buffer Folio hands it, instead of into a layer of its own (a new engine, engine 3, with a patch to mpv).
- **Picture in Picture starts and stops with nothing swapped.** Until now Metal moved mpv to a CPU renderer at each PiP start and back at the stop, and mpv rebuilt its video and seeked: the stall and the spinner.
- **No reload after Notification Centre, the app switcher or the Home Screen.** Leaving the app stops the GPU work (iOS refuses it in the background) and coming back goes on drawing. Until now Metal took its video output down and built it again on return, with a new decoder and a seek.
- **What Metal keeps over OpenGL:** Dolby Vision applied (no green, nothing stripped), 10-bit HDR straight from the hardware decoder (no CPU copy, so no 4K lag), libplacebo's scaling and Anime4K.
- Media Info says "Metal (libplacebo), video layer" when this path is in use. If the engine can't do it, Metal falls back to its own layer as before.

### Tested
