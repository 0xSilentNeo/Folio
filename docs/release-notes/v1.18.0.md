**Install:** download `Folio-1.18.0.ipa` below and sideload it.

Nothing in this build has been tested on a phone yet. Everything below was tested in the simulator only, and Picture in Picture can't run there.

### Search as you type in Discover
- **Titles appear while you type.** A search starts 0.3 s after you stop typing, from 2 letters on. Return searches whatever you've typed.
  - Titles come from IMDb, the app's usual source for title info.
  - Each new letter cancels the search before it, so an older answer can't replace a newer one.
- **Your addons are searched when you press Search,** not on every pause. They're other people's servers.
- **Recent searches.**
  - When you tap the search field before typing, your recent searches show.
  - While you type, the recent searches that match show above the live results.
  - A search is saved when you press Search or open a result. Searching the same thing again (any capitals or spacing) moves it to the top.
  - The list keeps 50 searches. Swipe one to delete it, or use Clear (it asks first).
- **When there's no answer:**
  - "No titles match"
  - "You're offline" with Try Again
  - "Couldn't reach IMDb" with Try Again
- **Scrolling hides the keyboard.** Your first tap on a result opens it, even with the keyboard up.
- **Tested in the simulator:**
  - Results appear without pressing Search.
  - The first tap with the keyboard up opens the title.
  - Recent searches work, including delete and Clear, and are still there after a relaunch.
  - Offline shows the message and Try Again works.
  - The recent-search list passes 10 unit tests: duplicates, order, the 50 limit, saving and reading back, a damaged file.

### The leaf in the app switcher, and its flash on the way back in
- **What it was:** iOS's launch screen, the leaf on the plain background. iOS shows it when it has no picture of the app the way round the app is coming back.
- **Why:**
  - iOS keeps one picture of the app, taken as it leaves. With the player in landscape, that picture is landscape.
  - Open the app switcher or the app with the phone upright, and the player came back upright.
  - iOS had no upright picture of it, so it showed the launch screen in the switcher card, and for a moment on the way in.
- **How I know:** the simulator does the same without PiP.
  - The files iOS keeps for Folio held only a landscape picture of the player.
  - The recording shows the leaf in the switcher card, and for about 0.3 s on the way back in.
  - Coming back in landscape, or with the player upright, iOS showed the real picture and there was no leaf.
- **Fixed:** while the app is away, the player keeps the way round it was left.
  - The switcher shows the player, and you go straight back into it.
  - Simulator: no leaf in the card or on the way in.
- **One change you'll notice:** coming back with the phone upright, the video stays in landscape until you turn the phone. From then on it follows the phone as before. Tell me if you'd rather it turned upright straight away. That brings back a turn right after you come in, but no leaf.
- **Not tested on a phone with PiP.** Please check:
  1. Play in landscape and swipe home so PiP starts.
  2. Open the app switcher with the phone upright: Folio's card should show the player ("Playing in Picture in Picture"), not the leaf.
  3. Tap it: no leaf before the video.

### The stutter coming back from Picture in Picture
- **Not fixed yet.** I need your phone's trace to find the cause; I won't guess it.
- **What your earlier logs already show (1.17.0–1.17.4):**
  - Coming back, the video output switches back in 51–61 ms.
  - In 3 of 15 returns, the picture then stood still within the first 3 s: for 0.2, 0.2 and 1.0 s. The other 12 had no still picture.
- **Coming back upright made the player rebuild its picture at a new size in the middle of the hand-back.** The fix above stops that, so it may help here too. Only the trace can tell.
- **To send me a trace:**
  1. Settings → Help & Diagnostics → Diagnostics → turn on **Trace Picture in Picture**.
  2. Play a video. Swipe home so PiP starts, wait 5 s, open the app the way you normally do. Do this 3 times, once with the phone upright and once sideways.
  3. Diagnostics → Share report, and send me the .zip.
  4. Turn the trace off afterwards.
- **What the trace records, step by step:**
  - iOS's PiP events
  - the video output switching
  - which picture is in front
  - every frame the video shows for 3 s
  - main-thread hitches
  - what the app looked like when iOS took its switcher picture
  - the pictures iOS kept

### Work the app did for nothing
- **Every closed video stayed in memory.** That included its video views and PiP layer. The player's menu opener held on to the whole session.
  - Simulator: after closing a video twice, neither session was freed before the fix. After the fix, both were freed 0.4 s after closing.
- **A title page's timer kept running under the video,** 10 times a second, for as long as you watched. It now stops while the player is up.
- **With PiP turned off, the app still copied the current frame after every seek and pause.** Only PiP uses that copy. It's skipped now when PiP is off.
- **Home worked out its library list again after every return to the app,** even when no file had changed. It doesn't now.
- **Each title page's Play button left four system listeners behind.** They go with it now.
