**Install:** download `Folio-1.14.1.ipa` below and sideload it.

### Player panels (iOS 26): no stutter, no waiting
- The panel button pill's glass grows into the panel (Tracks, Settings, Now Playing) and shrinks back into the pill when it closes
- No more video stutter: the picture used to be frozen for about half a second while a panel opened or closed. Nothing is frozen now
- Taps never wait for the animation: tap outside while a panel is still opening and it closes at once; tap the button again while it's closing and it comes straight back (it used to wait about a second for iOS's popover to finish)
- iOS 17 and 18 keep the previous panels unchanged

### Thumbnails
- The watched-progress bar sits inside the picture as a white capsule on a dark track, so it no longer blends into the cream page in light mode
