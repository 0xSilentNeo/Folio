*Renamed from v1.30.5 on 19 Nov 2026, when the version numbers started again at 0.90.0. The app in this build still calls itself 1.30.5.*

**Test build.** Download `Folio-1.30.5.ipa` below and sideload it. It updates 1.30.4. It's a pre-release until you've checked it on your phone.

### Playback: the stutters and broken picture of 1.30.4
- **Streams read steadily again.** 1.30.4 had mpv fill its buffer, then rest until 15 seconds were left, so the phone's radio could sleep. Every read after a rest began with corrupt data: your 16 Nov log has mpv's "Corrupt file detected" about every 95 seconds, each one a buffer (about 380 MB) after the last, and the hardware decoder failing on it 24 times in an evening (3 times on 1.30.3). Each failure was a freeze and a broken picture until the next keyframe. Folio reads as it did through 1.30.3 again, and the buffer is your setting again (1.30.4 raised it to at least 384 MB).
- The other battery changes of 1.30.4 stay: the video drawn at its own size (one GPU pass, under a millisecond a frame in your log), the AirPlay button that searches only when tapped, and no Real-Debrid re-listing while you watch.

### Search: Library and Search
- **Two tabs**: Library (what you have — your library, your servers, Plex and Jellyfin, the best match first with Play or Resume) and Search (movies and shows from IMDb and your addons). "All" is gone.
- **Shows and Movies** under Search narrow the list; with neither chosen you see everything. Search on the Search tab also asks IMDb's fuller title search for both, so a part of a name finds more than eight titles.
- **Folio remembers which tab you used**, Library or Search. Shows and Movies start fresh with each new search.
- When your tab has nothing and the other does, a line says so ("Nothing in your library — 12 results in Search"); a tap switches.

### Skip pills sooner
- The lookups now start when you press play, while the stream opens, instead of after: on 16 Nov the pills came about 1.4 s after a 6.5 s open. A prepared next episode has its pills looked up while it's being readied, so they're there when it opens.
- The first service to answer is shown at once (it waited up to 2 s for the other); a later answer is merged without moving a pill you're inside or one you've skipped.
- An intro or credits the services leave open-ended is no longer dropped when the file's length isn't known yet.

### AirPlay
- The route list should now grow out of the AirPlay button like the other panels (iOS draws it; it couldn't from an invisible picker).

### Tested
- Unit tests pass (767, 30 of them new: the search tabs, filter and hint, and the skip lookup's order, cache and merge).
- Simulator UI tests pass for search (including a new one for the tabs, the hint and the pills), the kids profile, Settings search, Home, the Real-Debrid row and launch.
- **Not checked on a phone:** streaming with steady reading again, the skip timing and the AirPlay animation. Nothing in this release was reviewed by a second model before release.

### Known and left
- 1.30.4's battery change to read in bursts is gone, so the radio is busy again while a stream plays; the other battery changes stay.
